# 01.04 Ordered Observation, Live Streaming, and Backpressure

Roadmap priority 4. Extends Phase 1 replayable observation into Phase 4 live model streams and later reconnectable clients.

## Purpose

Study how Go systems preserve ordering and terminal truth while isolating slow observers, bounding buffers, distinguishing durable facts from lossy deltas, and reconnecting without making observers control execution.

## Steps

- Trace event creation, sequencing, fan-out, buffering, replay, and subscriber removal.
- Separate lifecycle facts from progress, token deltas, logs, and other high-volume data.
- Inspect the policy for full queues, slow or disconnected consumers, and stream cancellation.
- Check whether producer goroutines perform blocking observer work or callbacks.
- Examine reconnect cursors, terminal delivery, dropped-event accounting, and memory bounds.

## Questions

- Can one slow observer delay execution or other observers?
- What is bounded, dropped, coalesced, retained, or replayed under pressure?
- How is exactly one terminal fact delivered after partial streaming?
- Does reconnect recover semantics or merely resume best-effort display data?
