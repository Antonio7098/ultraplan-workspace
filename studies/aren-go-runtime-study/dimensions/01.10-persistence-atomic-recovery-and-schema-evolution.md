# 01.10 Persistence, Atomic Recovery, and Schema Evolution

Roadmap priority 10. Covers Phase 11 only after the in-memory lifecycle and real execution path are understood.

## Purpose

Find storage patterns that persist Aren-owned state and events coherently, recover honestly after crashes, evolve formats safely, and avoid implying exactly-once external effects.

## Steps

- Trace write ordering among state, outcome, events, artifacts, checkpoints, and acknowledgements.
- Inspect WAL, transactional, atomic-file, sync, rename, and checkpoint mechanisms and their failure windows.
- Follow startup recovery through corruption handling, incomplete writes, interrupted work, and reconciliation.
- Examine schema and physical-format versioning, migrations, rollback limits, and compatibility tests.
- Find crash, fault-injection, replay, and durability tests, noting what storage guarantees they actually establish.

## Citations

- Aren Phase 11: ../../../../Aren/docs/phased-roadmap.md#phase-11--persistence-and-recovery
- Commit pipeline, WAL, manifest, checkpoint, migration, recovery, and corruption code.
- Tests that terminate or fault writes between each durable step.

## Questions

- What is the atomic unit, and does it match Aren-owned lifecycle state rather than external effects?
- After restart, how is interrupted work distinguished from failed or completed work?
- Can recovery duplicate a tool or remote side effect, and is that stated honestly?
- How does a newer binary refuse, migrate, or safely open older data?
