# 01.12 Policy, Admission, Scheduling, and Fairness

Roadmap priority 12. Covers Phase 13 governance before daemon hosting makes resource mistakes shared and long-lived.

## Purpose

Study pre-effect policy decisions, bounded admission, queue ownership, feasibility checks, reservations, fairness, retries, and observable rejection in mature Go schedulers and model servers.

## Steps

- Trace a request from admission through queueing, feasibility, reservation, dispatch, completion, and resource release.
- Distinguish policy denial, capacity rejection, transient wait, scheduling failure, and execution failure.
- Inspect priority, fairness, starvation protection, per-tenant or per-workspace limits, and queue bounds.
- Examine Ack, Nack, retry, blocked-work, and stale-plan behaviour.
- Check whether every governed effect has an observable decision made before it starts.

## Questions

- Can a request start before its policy and resources are committed?
- What prevents starvation when long and short runs share capacity?
- Who owns retry after a rejected or stale scheduling plan?
- Are queue depth and waiting time bounded and visible to callers?
