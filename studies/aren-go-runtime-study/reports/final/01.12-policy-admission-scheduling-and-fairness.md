## Executive Summary

Phase 13 governance asks one question with operational teeth: can a request start before its policy and resources are committed, and if not, who decides, who waits, and who retries? Nomad and Ollama answer from opposite ends of the scheduler spectrum and together define the design space for Aren.

Nomad is a cluster broker (`EvalBroker` → `Worker` → `Scheduler` → `PlanQueue` → Raft `planApply`) that enforces synchronous admission before replication — mutators then validators plus ACL/Sentinel at `Job.Register` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/job_endpoint.go:66-76`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/job_endpoint_hooks.go:178-192`) — then asynchronous scheduling with priority-ordered heaps, per-job serialization, `Ack`/`Nack` token delivery, a dedicated `BlockedEvals` cache, and optimistic pipelined `planApply` with `RefreshIndex` stale-plan fencing (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:182-189`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:542-560`). Every effect is gated by Raft commit and `AllocsFit`; every rejection is staged (`pending→blocked→failed`) and observable via `Eval.Status` + `PlanAnnotations`.

Ollama is a single-node model-residency scheduler concentrated in `server/sched.go` + `server/routes.go` that governs one scarce resource — VRAM and loaded-model slots. Admission is a two-stage gate — `scheduleRunner` policy checks then bounded `pendingReqCh` enqueue with immediate `503 ErrMaxQueue` on overflow (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:90-96`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:204-208`) — and feasibility is predictive (`PredictServerVRAM` vs `free*80/100` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:546-570`) corrected after spawn via one-shot OOM retry (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:627-674`). Dispatch is serialized through a single `processPending` goroutine with `activeLoading` single-slot (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:66-74`), residency via `refCount` + TTL timers (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:475-492`), and every rejection maps to a typed HTTP status (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:3107-3120`).

Convergence: both enforce "no start before commit" — capability/policy before queueing, feasibility before materialization, and ref-count or Raft token before dispatch. Both make rejection observable rather than silent. Both separate transport retry from semantic retry and both park unsatisfiable work instead of busy-looping (Nomad's `BlockedEvals`, Ollama's evict-and-wait loop).

Divergence is where Aren must choose: Nomad provides a durable, multi-queue broker with priority and blocked-state lifecycle but leaves queues unbounded and fairness at priority-only + random shuffle; Ollama bounds depth with drop-on-full but leaves wait time unbounded, FIFO only, no priority, no tenant quotas, and only one server-side retry. Neither provides tenant isolation, weighted fair queuing, aging, or bounded wait with `Retry-After` — precisely the Phase 13 gaps Aren must close before daemon hosting makes resource mistakes shared and long-lived.

## Core Thesis

**Governance is a staged pipeline with explicit handoffs, not a single admission check.** Sources that survive production separate five stages — policy denial → capacity rejection → transient wait → scheduling failure → execution failure — and assign ownership of queue, feasibility, reservation, retry, and release to different components with fencing between them.

Two archetypes emerge:

- **Nomad's durable broker + blocked cache** — admission is an in-process mutator/validator chain that fails fast without replication cost (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/job_endpoint_hooks.go:178-213`), queuing is a persistent priority heap with per-job serialization and `Ack`/`Nack` + `deliveryLimit` at-least-once delivery (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:599-675`), feasibility is a two-phase fit (`FeasibilityWrapper` cache at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/scheduler/feasible/feasible.go:1418-1554` then authoritative `AllocsFit` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:776-844`), and stale or unsatisfiable plans are parked in `BlockedEvals` with `unblockIndexes` and missed-unblock detection (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:359-417`) and re-enqueued event-driven. Strength: throughput via optimistic pipelining (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:100-243`) and correctness via `RefreshIndex` fencing. Cost: unbounded queues, priority-only fairness, enterprise-gated quota.

- **Ollama's single-node channel bus + predict-and-correct loader** — admission is `scheduleRunner` capability check then `select { pendingReqCh <- req : default: ErrMaxQueue }` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:204-208`), queuing is a single FIFO channel drained by one `processPending` goroutine (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:225-245`), feasibility is prediction (`predictedForLoad > free*80/100` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:546-570` + `selectLlamaServerPlacement` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:972-1031`) validated only after `WaitUntilRunning` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:627-674`), and retry is a bounded single OOM correction (`reduceAutoNumCtxForLoadOOM` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:757-782` + `evictAllAndWait` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1586-1630`). Strength: bounded depth, trivial correctness, no persistence. Cost: unbounded wait, head-of-line blocking, no fairness, driver-stale VRAM.

Aren's Phase 13 is structurally closer to Nomad's broker but operationally constrained like Ollama's single scarce resource (daemon capacity). It needs Nomad's staged decisions and fencing, Ollama's explicit bounded admission with observable `503`, and neither's fairness omissions — weighted fair queuing, aging, per-workspace quotas, and bounded wait must be added, not inherited.

## Pattern Catalog

### P1. Synchronous policy chain before replication/queuing

- **Problem:** policy checks that run after queuing waste queue slots, replication bandwidth, and scheduling cycles on requests that should never have been admitted.
- **Demonstrated by:** Nomad most explicitly — `admissionControllers` runs mutators then validators before `Evaluation` creation (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/job_endpoint_hooks.go:178-192`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/job_endpoint.go:132-249`) with volume/CSI ACL and `PolicyOverride` checks (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/job_endpoint.go:115-211,382-396`). Ollama mirrors it at smaller scale — `scheduleRunner` denies `nil` model, `CheckCapabilities`, projector guard before `getRunner` enqueue (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:202-232`).
- **Why it works:** fail-fast without Raft or channel occupancy; callers get typed errors (`400` vs `503` vs `500`) that distinguish policy denial from capacity rejection.
- **When to copy:** whenever admission is cheaper than scheduling — always for daemon `Job.Submit`-like RPCs where quota/capability denial should not consume `pendingReqCh`-like slots.
- **When overkill/risky:** checks require cluster state that is not locally available; then gate on local policy (capabilities, shape) and defer quota feasibility to the scheduler phase with observable `blocked` status.
- **Evidence:** Nomad's `registrationsAreAllowed` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/job_endpoint.go:1383-1396`) and Ollama's `ErrMaxQueue → 503` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:88,204-208`) are the same pattern at different stages — early deny, typed status.

### P2. Bounded admission with explicit drop vs unbounded availability queue

- **Problem:** unbounded queues bound correctness but not memory; saturated callers need backpressure, not silent queuing.
- **Demonstrated by:** Ollama explicitly — `pendingReqCh` capacity `OLLAMA_MAX_QUEUE=512` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/envconfig/config.go:279`) enforced as non-blocking `select default: ErrMaxQueue` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:204-208`) with test `TestSchedGetRunner` proving drop (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched_test.go:520-534`). Nomad demonstrates the anti-pattern — `ready`/`pending`/`PlanQueue`/`blockedEvals` grow unbounded, only `capacityChangeCh` buf 8096 and metric maps capped to 10k (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:337-339`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:21`), with coarse load-shedding `RejectJobRegistration` bool (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/structs/operator.go:230-232`).
- **Why it works / fails:** drop bounds handler goroutines and memory at cost of client retry; unbounded favors availability but risks OOM under burst, visible via `BrokerStats.TotalReady/Unacked/Pending/ByScheduler` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:1019-1033`) without admission enforcement.
- **When to copy Ollama's drop:** edge/daemon with limited memory where queuing 10k evals would OOM — return `429/503 + Retry-After` immediately.
- **When to copy Nomad's unbounded (with guard):** control-plane where Raft history already bounds retention and GC (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/core_sched.go:94-212`) provides reclamation; add `maxReady/maxPending/maxPlanDepth` with 429 before adopting unbounded.

### P3. Staged retry ownership: transport vs semantic vs parked

- **Problem:** conflating "RPC failed" with "placement infeasible" leads to tight retry loops that burn scheduler cycles or tight client loops that amplify load.
- **Demonstrated by:** Nomad separates three owners — Worker owns transport retry (`Plan.Submit` with `No cluster leader`/`plan queue is disabled` + `backoffErr` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/worker.go:689-700,875-885`), Scheduler owns semantic retry (`retryMax(limit, s.process, progressMade)` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/scheduler/generic_sched.go:137-160`, `missing state refresh after partial commit` via `RefreshIndex` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:545-556,709-723`), and `BlockedEvals` owns parked retry via `Unblock*` → `EnqueueAll` events (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:674-759`) plus broker `Nack` delay and `deliveryLimit → _failed` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:709-711`). Ollama splits differently — server retries once for OOM/prediction (`oomRetryAttempted` + `reduceAutoNumCtxForLoadOOM` + `evictAllAndWait` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:38-40,627-669,1586-1661`, `runtimeOOM` expire at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1632-1661`), client owns `ErrMaxQueue → 503` with no server requeue (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:204-208`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:3113-3114`), and stale plans self-correct inside `processPending` via `needsReload` + expire→wait→reload (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:255-264,1381-1431`).
- **Why it works:** each retry has the right signal — leader outage needs backoff, stale snapshot needs fresher state, infeasible needs capacity event, VRAM mispredict needs eviction. Mixing them causes thundering herd or livelock.
- **Copy:** assign `transport → worker/HTTP client with backoff`, `stale/prediction miss → scheduler with bounded one-retry + eviction`, `infeasible → blocked cache with event-driven unblock`. Never retry `ErrMaxQueue`/policy denial inside the server.

### P4. Blocked-work cache with missed-unblock fencing

- **Problem:** unsatisfiable requests should not spin the scheduler; but capacity events must not be missed while the scheduler is computing.
- **Demonstrated by:** Nomad's `BlockedEvals` is unusually clear — `processBlock` dedups per `NamespacedID` keeping higher `max(CreateIndex,SnapshotIndex)` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:289-343`), stores `ClassEligibility`/`Escaped`/`QuotaLimitReached`/`MissingResources`, `missedUnblock` compares `eval.SnapshotIndex` vs `unblockIndexes[class/quota/resource].index` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:359-417`) to immediately `EnqueueAll` if an unblock was missed during scheduling, and `Unblock*` stores index and sends `capacityUpdate` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:484-644`). Ollama lacks this; its equivalent is the `processPending` evict-then-loop with `unloadedCh` wait (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:338-358`) and no index — so cancellation while waiting holds the head slot.
- **Why it works:** parking replaces polling; the index fence closes the `schedule(Idx N)` → `capacity(N+1)` race that would otherwise leave evals stranded until next unrelated event (prune window `5m/15m` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:23-29` is the residual risk).
- **When to copy:** any admission that parks unsatisfiable work — daemon capacity, GPU slots, workspace concurrency caps. Use monotonic `Index` + per-class `unblockIndexes` map, not timestamp.
- **Overkill:** single-slot FIFO with immediate eviction (Ollama's current shape) where queue depth ≤ `MaxRunners` and eviction cost dominates — a simple `unloadedCh` drain suffices until concurrency grows.

### P5. Optimistic pipeline with authoritative fit check vs predict-then-correct

- **Problem:** pre-spawn feasibility is either stale or expensive; strict serialization kills throughput, pure prediction risks overcommit.
- **Demonstrated by:** Nomad pipelines 6-8 plans overlapping Raft latency, then re-validates against `SnapshotMinIndex(max(prevPlanIndex, plan.SnapshotIndex))` and per-node `AllocsFit` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:100-189,776-844`), returning `RefreshIndex` to force stale schedulers to retry — optimistic for throughput, authoritative for safety. Ollama predicts `PredictServerVRAM` + `availableMemoryForPlacement` at `80%` headroom (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:547-550,972-1031`) plus `updateFreeSpace` reconciliation (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1295-1335`), then spawns and corrects after `WaitUntilRunning` log parsing, with 75% reclamation wait (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1441-1499`) — prediction for availability, correction for driver lag.
- **Why it works / tradeoffs:** pipeline gains throughput but increases stale work and `RejectedNodes`; 80% + 60/75% batch headroom and `mmapHostPressureHeadroom = max(8 GiB, total/10)` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:887-895,1255-1260`) wastes VRAM to avoid OOM churn. No adaptive tuning from `memoryParsingWriter` is present.
- **Copy for Aren:** use Nomad's fence — schedulers run against snapshot, commit checks `AllocsFit` under monotonic index — for daemon capacity. For VRAM-like prediction, keep headroom but feed observed `VRAMByGPU` back into next prediction; do not retry prediction misses more than once without eviction.

### P6. Observable staged decisions with typed rejection

- **Problem:** callers cannot distinguish "fix your request" from "try again later" from "no capacity" without typed status and waiting-time visibility.
- **Demonstrated by:** Nomad surfaces `EvalStatus pending/blocked/complete/failed` + `FailedTGAllocs` + `AllocMetric.FilteredNodes` + `PlanAnnotations` via `SetStatus` → `ReblockEval`/`UpdateEval`/`CreateEval` → `EvalUpdateRequestType` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/worker.go:728-870`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/scheduler/generic_sched.go:161-177`) and metrics `BrokerStats`/`BlockedStats`/`QueueStats.Depth`/`wait_time` histograms (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:1019-1033,984-1016`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals_stats.go:23-70`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_queue.go:201-217`). Ollama maps `errCapabilities→400`, `context.Canceled→499`, `ErrMaxQueue→503`, `os.ErrNotExist→404` via `handleScheduleError` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:3107-3120`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:467-473`) and exposes `PsHandler` loaded models but not `pendingReqCh` depth (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:2271-2303`).
- **Why it matters for Aren:** Phase 13 requires every governed effect to have an observable decision *before* it starts. Status + filtered-nodes + depth + wait-time histograms must be first-class API/headers, not logs.
- **Gap to close:** both lack `Retry-After`; Nomad lacks bounded-depth 429; Ollama lacks any depth gauge. Adopt Nomad's metrics, Ollama's typed HTTP mapping, and add both missing pieces.

### P7. Tokenized at-least-once vs channel-trio ownership (Ack/Nack discipline)

- **Problem:** dequeued work must not be lost on worker crash, nor duplicated silently.
- **Demonstrated by:** Nomad's `Dequeue → token + NackTimer → OutstandingReset → Ack/Nack/{Pause,Resume}NackTimeout` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:493-525,599-772`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/worker.go:650-673`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_endpoint.go:70-73`) with Raft-index coupling via `plan.SnapshotIndex`/`waitIndex`, `deliveryLimit`, and `failedQueue="_failed"` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:29,53-55`). Ollama's trio — `pendingReqCh` (admission), `finishedReqCh` (release), `expiredCh`/`unloadedCh` (reclamation) at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:61-65` — partitions ownership between `processPending` and `processCompleted` with `refCount` lease (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:475-492,367-413`), but `expiredCh` sends hold `refMu` with bounded 512 buffer (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1726-1727`), risking block if `processCompleted` stalls in `waitForVRAMRecovery` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:414-467`).
- **When to copy Nomad's token model:** any broker where dequeue and commit are separate Raft commits — pause timers around `PlanQueue` to avoid premature Nack.
- **When channel trio suffices:** single-process daemon where `processPending`/`processCompleted` are the only owners and crash loses only in-memory queue — simpler but must never hold locks across channel sends or head-of-line wait must be `ctx`-interruptible.

## Key Differences

**Queue existence and bounds.** Nomad has four queues — broker `ready`/`pending`, `PlanQueue`, and `BlockedEvals` — all unbounded, with metrics but no admission rejection on depth; shedding is coarse `RejectJobRegistration` bool (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/structs/operator.go:230-232`). Ollama has one bounded queue `pendingReqCh=512` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/envconfig/config.go:279`) that rejects synchronously (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:204-208`). Divergence reflects product shape: Nomad's control-plane persists evals to Raft and expects operators to scale schedulers; Ollama's desktop server must bound handler goroutines. For Aren, both extremes are wrong — depth must be bounded like Ollama, but with per-workspace partitions like neither provides.

**Reservation primitive.** Nomad has no `reservation` lock distinct from allocation — capacity is checked via `AllocsFit` including proposed allocs then atomically committed via `ApplyPlanResults` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:389-400`); schedulers never hold VRAM-like locks. Ollama reserves via `activeLoading` single-slot plus `refCount++` and `expireTimer` pin (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:517-523,475-492`) — a lease, not a Raft commit. One is ledger-committed, one is ref-count-leased; a daemon needs both — a short `reservation` (lease) between feasibility and dispatch, then commit.

**Fairness and starvation.** Nomad: strict priority + per-job serialization + random shuffle/seeded Fisher-Yates (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:1040-1091`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/scheduler/feasible/feasible.go:131-165`), per-job `pending` heap prevents single-job monopolization but cross-job starvation is unbounded, no aging, no WFQ, no quota in OSS (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply_ce.go:27-30`). Ollama: no priority at all, strict FIFO channel (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:233`), `ByDurationAndName` LRU eviction (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1557-1578`) that ignores request age, treats `KeepAlive=-1 → MaxInt64` as last-to-evict (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/envconfig/config.go:140`) allowing one pinned model to starve all subsequent loads, no `priority` field on `LlmRequest` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:29-58`). Both fail the dimension's starvation question for opposite reasons; the common lesson is that priority without aging still starves and FIFO without priority also starves — aging or WFQ is required.

**Stale-plan handling.** Nomad fences with `RefreshIndex`/`SnapshotIndex` comparison and optimistic re-validation; stale triggers `!fullCommit` → `retryMax` re-process (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/scheduler/generic_sched.go:307-322`). Ollama fences only via `needsReload` deep-equals + `Ping` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1381-1431`) and `activeLoading` panic guard (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:599-605`), with no `SnapshotIndex`/`RefreshIndex` token — `pid` + `modelKey` equality is the only guard (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:441-451`). Nomad's index fence survives restarts; Ollama's pid guard does not.

**Waiting-time obligations.** Nomad bounds waiting via `Wait` + `WaitUntil` on `delayHeap` plus `runDelayedEvalsWatcher` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:283-293,886-936`), Nack compounding delays, and `NackTimeout` with pause/resume — visible via `enqueuedTime`/`dequeuedTime` histograms (10k cap at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:337-339`) and `deliveryLimit`. Ollama has no per-request deadline, no `Wait`/`WaitUntil`, no Nack timeout — wait is sum of prior pending × (`OLLAMA_LOAD_TIMEOUT` default 5 m at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/envconfig/config.go:150` + `waitForVRAMRecovery` 5 s + `evictAllAndWait` per-runner drain), blocked on `unloadedCh` without `ctx` select (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:350-358`), so a cancelled head still delays the line. Both tax long vs short mixing, but Nomad makes wait observable while Ollama does not.

**Operational visibility.** Nomad emits `BrokerStats{TotalReady,TotalUnacked,TotalPending,TotalWaiting,TotalCancelable,DelayedEvals,ByScheduler}` + `BlockedStats` + `QueueStats.Depth` + `wait_time/process_time/response_time` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:1019-1033`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:867-905`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_queue.go:201-217`). Ollama exposes only loaded-model `PsHandler` and `TotalDuration`/`LoadDuration` on success — no pending depth gauge, no wait histogram (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:2271-2303,709-710`). One over-instruments, one under-instruments; Aren needs Nomad's depth signals on Ollama's admission boundary.

## Tradeoffs

**Optimistic pipelining (throughput) vs strict serial apply (freshness).** Nomad's `planApply` 6-8 in-flight (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:100-243`) overlaps Raft latency but increases snapshot staleness and `RejectedNodes`/`RefreshIndex` churn. Benefit: 6-8× commit throughput. Cost: wasted scheduler cycles and `retryMax` re-runs under load. Best-fit: clusters with bursty job submissions where Raft is the bottleneck. Alternative: Ollama's `activeLoading` single-slot (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:69-74`) serializes perfectly fresh `updateFreeSpace` per load — zero staleness, minimal throughput. For Aren's daemon, default to serialized reservations (Ollama) for VRAM-like scarce resources and pipelined Raft (Nomad) only for persistent job state where throughput matters.

**Priority heap + random tie-break (simplicity) vs weighted fair queuing (isolation).** Strict priority with `rand.Intn(n)` cross-scheduler tie-break (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:474-488`) and node shuffle seeded by `EvalID` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/scheduler/feasible/feasible.go:131-165`) is trivial to reason about and favors latency for high priority. Cost: low-priority batch/starved indefinitely behind churn; no per-tenant bound, quota-gated only in enterprise. WFQ/leasing alternative would prevent starvation at cost of priority Inversion complexity and bookkeeping. Neither source implements it; Aren must.

**Drop-on-full 503 (handler safety) vs blocking with deadline (SLO).** Ollama's drop (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:204-208`) bounds goroutines at client-retry cost without `Retry-After`. Nomad's unbounded wait with `Wait`/`WaitUntil` + `delayHeap` + `PauseNackTimeout` keeps handlers blocked but honors dwell visibility. Benefit of drop: memory-safe under burst. Benefit of wait: tail latency is server-owned and measurable. Best-fit: daemon API should drop with `429 + Retry-After` for admission overflow (Ollama) but support `WaitUntil` with deadline for capacity-waiting (Nomad's `BlockedEvals` style) on the same endpoint — distinguish overload from transient capacity wait.

**Predictive headroom (utilization) vs authoritative ledger (waste).** `80%` VRAM threshold plus `60%/75%` batch and `max(8 GiB, total/10)` mmap headroom (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:547-550,887-895,1255-1260`) absorb driver lag but wastes VRAM; no adaptive feedback from `memoryParsingWriter`. `AllocsFit` ledger check (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:842`) wastes no capacity but requires Raft index fencing and scheduler work on stale data. Predictive suits driver-reported memory with lag; ledger suits allocator-owned memory. Aren needs ledger for daemon concurrency slots and predictive only where driver/host reports lag — never mispredict without one bounded correction.

**Per-job serialization (consistency) vs parallelism (scale).** Nomad's one-ready + pending heap per `NamespacedID` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:320-350`) prevents duplicate schedulers on same job (the `BlockedEvals` duplicate test at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals_test.go:70-78` proves correctness) but serializes deployment scaling for large jobs. No per-job parallelism option. Ollama serializes globally (`processPending` single goroutine), not per-model, so two independent models cannot load concurrently even on disjoint GPUs. Both serialize more than needed — Aren should shard by workspace/model for daemon capacity while serializing per-model like Ollama to avoid concurrent `llama-server` wrestle.

**Bounded single OOM retry vs compounding Nack backoff.** Ollama's `oomRetryAttempted` once (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:38-40`) with `32768→4096` step (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:917-926`) covers auto-context overcommit; explicit large `num_ctx` then fails fast. Nomad's `initialNackDelay` then `prevDequeues*subsequentNackDelay` compounding (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:728-738`) spreads transient failures but not fairness. One is minimal because each retry is a process restart; one compounds because each retry is a heap requeue. Match retry shape to cost: heavyweight spawns → one retry with eviction; lightweight requeues → exponential backoff with jitter and `deliveryLimit`.

## Decision Guide

**Choose the governance substrate by answering five questions:**

1. **Is the governed resource ledger-owned or driver-reported?** Ledger (allocator, concurrency slots, workspace quota) → Nomad's `AllocsFit` + `RefreshIndex` pattern (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:776-844,542-512`). Driver/host reported (VRAM, GPU free mem, host memory pressure) → Ollama's predict-then-correct with `updateFreeSpace` reconciliation (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1295-1335`) plus one bounded correction before surfacing `500`.

2. **Must admission shed or queue?** "Caller can back off, daemon memory is bounded" → Ollama's `pendingReqCh` drop with `503/429 + Retry-After` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:204-208`). "Caller expects eventual placement, requests are durable" → Nomad's `EvalBroker` + `BlockedEvals` parking with event-driven `EnqueueAll` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:674-759`). For Aren, do both: bounded admission at the daemon edge (drop), parked wait inside per-workspace blocked set (queue).

3. **Who retries?** Transport/leader loss → worker with `backoffErr` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/worker.go:875-885`). Stale snapshot/prediction miss → scheduler once with fresher state (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/scheduler/generic_sched.go:137-160`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:627-669`). Infeasible capacity → blocked cache until `capacityUpdate` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:484-644`); queue-full/policy denial → client only, no server requeue (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:3113-3114`). If you cannot name the owner for a failure class, you are missing a retry policy.

4. **Do long and short share capacity without priority isolation?** If yes, you must add what neither source has — aging or WFQ. Nomad's priority-only (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:1040-1091`) and Ollama's FIFO (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:233`) both admit starvation. Decision rule: if `sessionDuration`/`KeepAlive` can be infinite (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/envconfig/config.go:140`) or `Priority` is user-controlled, require weighted fair queue per workspace or an aging boost after `wait_time > threshold` using `enqueuedTime` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:403-413`).

5. **Is stale state dangerous?** If concurrent writes can reallocate the same capacity (node free, VRAM, slot), require Nomad's fencing token (`SnapshotIndex`/`RefreshIndex` + `max(prevPlanIndex, planSnapshotIndex)` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:182-189`) not Ollama's pid guard. If single-threaded `processPending` already serializes, a fencing token is overkill — but then you must accept throughput = 1 load at a time.

**When to borrow from each source:**

- From Nomad: the five-stage decision pipeline, `EvalBroker`'s per-job pending heap + `Ack`/`Nack` token + `deliveryLimit`→`_failed` discipline, `BlockedEvals` missed-unblock index fence, `PlanQueue` priority ordering, `RefreshIndex` stale-plan contract, and `BrokerStats`/`BlockedStats` metrics.
- From Ollama: the two-stage synchronous gate (`scheduleRunner` before `pendingReqCh`), immediate `ErrMaxQueue` typed mapping, `activeLoading` single-slot discipline where driver lag makes concurrency unsafe, `needsReload` auto-vs-explicit exemption (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1399-1407`), and `waitForVRAMRecovery` polling pattern (but with `ctx` interruptibility added).

**Do not borrow yet:** Nomad's unbounded queue growth without depth admission; Ollama's head-of-line `unloadedCh` wait that ignores `pending.ctx` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:350-358`); either source's fairness (none); enterprise-gated quota stubs (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply_ce.go:27-30`) as a model for workspace isolation — design workspace quotas as first-class, locally enforced, tested limits.

## Practical Tips

1. **Gate every effect with a decision record before it starts.** Replicate Nomad's `admissionControllers` before `Evaluation` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/job_endpoint_hooks.go:178-192`) and Ollama's `scheduleRunner` before `pendingReqCh` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:202-232`): policy check → bounded enqueue → feasibility → reservation → dispatch. No inference, alloc, or spawn begins without a logged `status` (`pending→blocked→complete`) and, for capacity wait, a wait token.

2. **Bound depth and expose it.** Size the admission queue (Ollama's `OLLAMA_MAX_QUEUE` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/envconfig/config.go:279`) and return `429/503` with `Retry-After` on drop; emit `QueueStats.Depth` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_queue.go:201-217`) and `BrokerStats.TotalReady/Pending/Waiting/ByScheduler` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:1019-1033`) as gauges, not logs. Alert on depth approaching limit before clients see `503`.

3. **Separate retry owners and cap them.** Transport → exponential `backoffErr` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/worker.go:875-911`); stale → one re-invoke with fresh snapshot (`retryMax` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/scheduler/generic_sched.go:137-160`); capacity → parked `BlockedEvals` until `Unblock*`; queue-full → client only (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:204-208`). Enforce `deliveryLimit` → `_failed` queue (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:709-711`) and Ollama's `oomRetryAttempted` once (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:38-40`); heavyweight restarts get one retry, lightweight requeues get backoff.

4. **Park, don't poll, and fence the unblock race.** Implement `BlockedEvals` with per-class `unblockIndexes` and `missedUnblock` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:359-417`) keyed by monotonic index, not wall time. On park, keep higher `max(CreateIndex,SnapshotIndex)` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:289-343`); on capacity event, `Unblock*` stores index then signals `capacityChangeCh` buffer 8096 (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:21`), and always check missed before sleeping.

5. **Make the reservation distinct from admission and revocable.** Hold a short lease (like `activeLoading` + `refCount` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:69-74,475-492` but with fencing token) between feasibility and `WaitUntilRunning`/Raft commit. Release on `pending.ctx.Done()` via `finishedReqCh` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:487-491`) and decrement `refCount` in `processCompleted` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:383-413`); never spawn without the lease.

6. **Predict only where you must, and feed correction back.** Where capacity is driver-reported, keep `PredictServerVRAM` + `80%` guard (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:547-550`) but after `WaitUntilRunning` feed observed `VRAMByGPU` via `memoryParsingWriter` into the next `updateFreeSpace` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1295-1335`). For ledger-owned capacity, use `AllocsFit` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_apply.go:842`) and never retry explicit large requests with automatic reduction — `numCtxAuto` guard (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:758-760`) is the correct fail-closed behavior.

7. **Shard the single `processPending` bottleneck.** Ollama's single goroutine (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:225-245`) head-blocks all pending behind one `waitForVRAMRecovery` (5 s) or `evictAllAndWait` per-runner drain (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1586-1630`). For Aren, keep single-slot per scarce device (GPU/model) but allow concurrent pending processing across independent pools; select `case <-pending.ctx.Done(): continue` alongside `case <-unloadedCh:` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:350-358`) to free the head when the waiter cancels.

8. **Never hold `refMu` across a bounded channel send.** `expireRunner` doing `s.expiredCh <- runner` under `refMu` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1726-1727`) while `processCompleted` may block in `waitForVRAMRecovery` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:458-464`) deadlocks the 512 buffer. Copy Nomad's `PauseNackTimeout`/`ResumeNackTimeout` discipline (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_endpoint.go:70-73`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:740-772`): release locks before send, or size the channel to `pending+loaded` and monitor depth.

9. **Map stage to HTTP status explicitly.** Follow `handleScheduleError` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/routes.go:3107-3120`): policy/capability → `400`, queue-full → `503/429 + Retry-After`, not-found → `404`, cancelled → `499`, execution OOM → `500` with structured `InsufficientCapacity` including `predicted/available` estimates (currently only logs at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:551-569`). Also surface `Wait`/`WaitUntil` deadline vs immediate `503` as distinct codes.

10. **Add the missing fairness now, before multi-tenant:** extend `LlmRequest` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:29-58`) or `Evaluation` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/job_endpoint.go:304-322`) with `priority` and replace FIFO/strict-priority with a bounded priority heap (Nomad's `ReadyEvaluations`/`PendingEvaluations` at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:1040-1091` constrained by per-workspace `maxPending` + WFQ/Aging), leveraging `enqueuedTime`/`schedAttempts` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:337-339`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:36`) as age signals. Test with saturation and starvation cases (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals_test.go:70-78,285-331`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched_test.go:1221-1372`).

## Anti-Patterns / Caution Signs

- **Unbounded broker queues with no 429.** Ready/pending/plan/blocked growth without depth admission (Nomad at `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/eval_broker.go:53-122`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/plan_queue.go:32-40`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/blocked_evals.go:17-29`) relies on GC (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/nomad/nomad/core_sched.go:94-212`) and operator `RejectJobRegistration` rather than per-workspace backpressure. Caution sign: `BrokerStats.TotalReady` grows monotonically under load without any `503`; fix with `maxReady/maxPending/maxPlanDepth` and client-visible `Retry-After`.

- **FIFO starvation disguised as fairness.** Ollama's `pendingReqCh` FIFO (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:233`) and `ByDurationAndName` that sorts `KeepAlive=-1 → MaxInt64` last but still pins VRAM (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1557-1578`, `studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/envconfig/config.go:140`) lets a burst of large `num_ctx=262144` loads occupy all `MaxRunners` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:265-291`) and starve short requests until `ErrMaxQueue` makes starvation visible as `503`. Caution sign: pending depth oscillates at limit while p50 wait stays flat but p99 diverges — add WFQ/aging.

- **Head-of-line wait that ignores cancellation.** `processPending` selects `case <-unloadedCh: continue` without `case <-pending.ctx.Done()` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:350-358`) and `TestSchedAlreadyCanceled` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched_test.go:1191-1207`) only covers dequeue-time cancel, not mid-wait cancel. A cancelled waiter still holds head until prior `evictAllAndWait` (`studies/aren-go-runtime-study/sources/ollama/server/sched.go:1619-1628`) and `waitForVRAMRecovery` (5 s) finish, delaying all later pending. Require `ctx`-aware wait on every `unloadedCh` drain.

## Per-Source Notes

### nomad (6/10)
Mature `Job.Register → EvalBroker → Worker → Scheduler → PlanQueue → planApply → Raft` pipeline where mutators/validators + Sentinel/ACL deny before replication, `EvalBroker` per-job ready slot + priority heaps preserve order and prevent duplicate scheduling, `BlockedEvals` parks unsatisfiable evals with `ClassEligibility/Escaped/QuotaLimitReached` and `missedUnblock` fencing, and `planApply` pipelines with `SnapshotMinIndex`/`RefreshIndex` stale handling and authoritative `AllocsFit`. Strong on "no start before commit" (optimistic snapshot + Raft-commit reservation), explicit `Ack`/`Nack` ownership with compounding delays and `cancelable` promotion, and observability (`BrokerStats`/`BlockedStats`/`FilteredNodes`/`PlanAnnotations`). Limited by unbounded queues (no depth 429, coarse `RejectJobRegistration`), priority-only fairness (random shuffle is anti-starvation), and OSS quota invisibility; duplication coalescing and `waitForSchedulers` gating are correct but class-coarse unblock can herd.

### ollama (4/10)
Minimal single-node channel scheduler correctly concentrated for one scarce resource: `scheduleRunner` capability gate → `pendingReqCh` 512 drop → `processPending` serialized feasibility (`PredictServerVRAM` vs `80%` free, `selectLlamaServerPlacement`, `updateFreeSpace`) → `activeLoading` single-slot `Load` → `WaitUntilRunning` → `refCount` pin → `finishedReqCh`/`expiredCh`/`unloadedCh` trio with TTL reclamation and `waitForVRAMRecovery` polling. Every effect is gated (no tokens before `runnerRef`), every rejection is typed (`400/404/503/500/499`), `needsReload` handles stale, and one OOM correction (`reduceAutoNumCtxForLoadOOM` 32768→4096 then `evictAllAndWait`) covers auto-config mispredict. Insufficient as governance substrate: no priority/FIFO only, no tenant quotas, head-of-line blocking, unbounded per-pending wait, `expiredCh` lock-channel coupling, invisible pending depth, and no `Retry-After` — a solid model-residency pattern, not a multi-tenant fairness layer.
