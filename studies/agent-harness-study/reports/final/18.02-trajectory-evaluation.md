## Executive Summary

Trajectory evaluation — *can you tell whether the agent took a good path even if the final answer was wrong?* — is absent or outsourced in most harnesses and only partially mature in two.

No source scores all four rubric questions (intermediate steps, tool choice, context usage, recovery). The two 6/10 leaders share a design choice others avoid: **derive trajectory scores from a captured structured record** rather than from final output alone. pydantic-ai captures an OTel `SpanTree` per case and runs deterministic span evaluators against it (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/otel/span_tree.py:29-404`, `pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:189-366`). agent-framework captures the full `conversation: list[Message]` on `EvalItem` and derives `query`/`response` through a pluggable `ConversationSplitter` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:76-107`, `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:254-296`). Both cleanly separate *path correctness* from *answer correctness* — a wrong `ctx.output` can still yield `TrajectoryMatch` F1=1.0 or pass `tool_calls_present`.

The remaining six diverge by *what they do instead of scoring trajectories*:

- **crewai (5) and openhands (5)** provide LLM-judge trajectory signals but couple them to expensive, fragile, opt-in paths. crewai isolates six LLM judges behind `crewai.experimental.evaluation` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/agent_evaluator.py:344-365`); the production `Crew.test()` never sees them (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/crew.py:2227-2253` uses only `CrewEvaluator`). openhands re-scores the entire prefix on every action via a remote `/classify` service (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/impl/api/client.py:262`, `_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/base.py:62` `mode=all_actions` vs default `finish_and_message`), collapsing tool/reasoning/context errors into one `success` probability.

- **langgraph (4)** is observability-rich, evaluation-poor. Checkpoints, `StateSnapshot`/`get_state_history`, and six `StreamMode`s (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:120-134`) let any external scorer reconstruct the path exactly, but the only in-repo trajectory scorers are notebook demos calling LangSmith `evaluate()` (`examples/rag/langgraph_crag_local.ipynb:724-745`).

- **langfuse (3), letta (2), openai-agents-sdk (2)** have no trajectory concept at all. langfuse has a durable single-score-per-execution pipeline (`worker/src/features/evaluation/evalService.ts:1039-1151`, `worker/src/features/evaluation/observationEval/observationEvalProcessor.ts:94-267`) that can score *one* observation or *one* trace holistically, never an ordered sequence. letta and openai-agents-sdk have rich step telemetry (`letta/schemas/step.py:16-66` `Step`/`StepStatus`, `src/agents/tracing/create.py:89-350` span hierarchy) but zero scoring — recovery, tool choice, and context quality are instrumented or retried, never graded.

Context usage and recovery are the systematic absences: no source ships a context-window utilization, retrieval-precision, or recovery-success evaluator. Where counts exist (token usage, `ContextWindowOverview`, `StepMetrics`) they are reported as metrics, not scored (`letta/schemas/memory.py:23-60`, `pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/_task_run.py:59-75`, `src/agents/usage.py:195-229`).

## Core Thesis

Trajectory evaluation maturity does not track observability maturity. Every studied harness *can* reconstruct a trajectory (spans, checkpoints, steps, events), but only two turn that reconstruction into a *scored trajectory* inside the repo.

The convergent lesson, visible despite different implementations, is that trajectory scoring requires **an explicit trajectory record as first-class input** and **a combiner that preserves step identity**. Where that record is explicit, deterministic scoring is cheap and testable. Where it is implicit (final output only, whole-history LLM prompt, or per-observation isolation), scoring either does not exist or requires re-assembling context the evaluator does not have.

Three invariants separate the 6s from the rest:

1. **Factoring is scoring.** agent-framework's `ConversationSplitter` and `EvalItem.per_turn_items()` and pydantic-ai's `_extract_tool_calls` sorted by `start_timestamp` both treat factoring (which turns/which spans constitute *the trajectory*) as part of the evaluation API. Langfuse's flat `{{var}}` substitution (`worker/src/features/evaluation/evalRuntime.ts:14-26`) and OpenHands' prefix re-scoring (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/critic_mixin.py:49-72`) do not — they score *whatever bytes they are given* as one blob.

2. **Tool identity can be scored deterministically without LLMs.** pydantic-ai's `ToolCorrectness` (multiset Counter, `allow_extra`), `TrajectoryMatch` (LCS F1), and `ArgumentCorrectness` (`subset` vs `exact` at `pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:384-502`) and agent-framework's `tool_calls_present`/`tool_call_args_match` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:1173-1277`) show that name + args + order can be scored with partial credit. Every LLM-only scorer (crewai, openhands, langfuse custom prompts) pays token cost and parse fragility for less reproducibility.

3. **Scoring and actuation must be separable.** openhands blurs evaluation and control (`CriticResult.score` directly drives `_check_iterative_refinement` at `_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/critic_mixin.py:76`); langgraph and openai-agents-sdk keep resilience (`RetryPolicy`, `model_retry.py:389-505`) entirely operational with no scorer observing it. The result in both cases is that recovery quality is unmeasurable — either hidden behind a retry or hidden behind a score that also triggers retry.

The divergence is not simply "mature vs immature" but product-shape-driven: platforms (langfuse) optimize for per-entity generic evaluation at ingestion scale and deliberately avoid graph semantics; graph layers (langgraph) keep scoring out to stay provider-agnostic and delegate to LangSmith; server-frontended frameworks (openhands) bias to a single remote classifier; while libraries that own both agent and eval (pydantic-ai, agent-framework) can afford to ship evaluators that read their own instrumentation.

## Approach Models

Sources cluster into five approach models. Rating is not a linear function of model — execution quality within a model dominates.

**Model A — Conversation/Span record + deterministic sequence scoring (agent-framework, pydantic-ai).** Both define one canonical trajectory record captured outside the evaluator and score it without LLMs. agent-framework stores `EvalItem.conversation` and derives `query`/`response` via `ConversationSplitter` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:76-178`), adding `per_turn_items()` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:254-296`) and workflow per-agent decomposition (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:941-1009`, `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:2031-2022`). pydantic-ai captures an OTel `SpanTree` per `Case` via `context_subtree` (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/dataset.py:970-988`, `pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/_task_run.py:35-77`) and scores it with multiset/F1/argument evaluators (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:189-502`). Both handle version skew by duplicating instrumentation constants (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:65-89`; agent-framework's `_resolve_function_args` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:1296-1351`). `HasMatchingSpan` with `SpanQuery` (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/otel/span_tree.py:29-404`) is the closest to a generic step-rule engine in the study.

**Model B — Experimental / holistic LLM-judge trajectory scoring (crewai, openhands).** Both score trajectories by asking an LLM about them, but differently. crewai's `EvaluationTraceCallback` singleton builds `{tool_uses, llm_calls}` traces from `ToolUsage*Event`/`LLMCall*Event` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/evaluation_listener.py:58-146`) and fans out six `BaseEvaluator.evaluate(agent, execution_trace, final_output, task)` judges (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/base_evaluator.py:62-69`, `lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/agent_evaluator.py:248-294`, `lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/metrics/tools_metrics.py:22-458`). openhands' `CriticBase.evaluate(events, git_patch) -> CriticResult` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/base.py:83`) posts the whole `View.from_events(events).events` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/impl/api/critic.py:85`) to a remote classifier returning ~22 label probs (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/impl/api/taxonomy.py:8-39`, `_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/impl/api/client.py:262`). crewai separates tool metrics into three judges; openhands collapses everything into one `success` score — the main intra-model difference.

**Model C — Observability-rich transport, scoring delegated externally (langgraph, openai-agents-sdk).** Both emit fine-grained, typed run topology and leave scoring to another system. langgraph's checkpointed state graph produces `StateSnapshot`/`CheckpointPayload`/`TaskPayload` and six `StreamMode`s (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:120-218`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/debug.py:41-279`) with time-travel via `get_state`/`get_state_history` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1391-1552`). openai-agents-sdk's span hierarchy `trace() -> task_span -> agent_span -> turn_span -> generation/function/guardrail/handoff_spans` (`src/agents/tracing/create.py:124-350`, `src/agents/tracing/span_data.py:28-266`) mirrors the run loop and is asserted via parent_id chains in tests (`tests/test_agent_tracing.py:99-185`). Neither has a `TrajectoryScorer` interface; docs point to LangSmith (`examples/rag/langgraph_crag_local.ipynb:806`) and OpenAI Evals (`docs/index.md:9`, `docs/multi_agent.md:39`) respectively.

**Model D — Platform generic evaluation (langfuse).** Langfuse scores one entity per job: whole trace, single named observation, or dataset item (`worker/src/features/evaluation/evalService.ts:1039-1151` vs `worker/src/features/evaluation/observationEval/observationEvalProcessor.ts:94-267`). Variable mapping (`packages/shared/src/features/evals/types.ts:88-107` with `jsonSelector`) and `compileEvalPrompt` (`worker/src/features/evaluation/evalRuntime.ts:14-26`) render a single LLM prompt; `buildDeterministicEvalScoreIds` (`packages/shared/src/server/evals/evalScoreIds.ts:6-38`) deduplicates atomically. Trajectory-aware scoring would require joining observations — intentionally unsupported to keep evaluation scalable and storage-agnostic.

**Model E — Performance telemetry without semantic judgment (letta).** Letta's `Step` (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/letta/letta/schemas/step.py:16-66`)/`StepMetrics` (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/letta/letta/schemas/step_metrics.py:13-26`)/`ProviderTrace` (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/letta/letta/schemas/provider_trace.py:21-86`)/`ContextWindowOverview` (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/letta/letta/schemas/memory.py:23-60`) plus `trace_method` OTEL (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/letta/letta/otel/tracing.py:228-435`) provide the studied set's most complete *structural* context measurement — every context section tokenized via `ContextWindowCalculator` (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/letta/letta/services/context_window_calculator/context_window_calculator.py:249-384`) — but strictly for execution/compaction, never for evaluation. The only quality signal is binary human `feedback` on `Step` via `PATCH /v1/steps/{step_id}/feedback` (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/letta/letta/services/step_manager.py:268-280`).

## Pattern Catalog

**P1. Trajectory as explicitly captured record + factoring function.**
- *Problem:* Final output conflates lucky paths and sound reasoning; scoring final output cannot answer "good path, wrong answer".
- *Demonstrated by:* agent-framework `EvalItem.conversation` + `ConversationSplitter` protocol + `per_turn_items()` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:76-296`); pydantic-ai `EvaluatorContext._span_tree` via `context_subtree`/`_task_run.TaskRun` (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/dataset.py:970-988`, `pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/context.py:30-103`); langgraph checkpoints/state snapshots (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:643-661`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1501-1552`). Openhands' `View.from_events` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/impl/api/critic.py:85`) is a variant that re-creates history from filtered events.
- *Why it works:* One canonical record decouples execution from judgment; evaluators become pure functions over that record, enabling offline re-scoring and transport-agnostic testing.
- *When to copy:* Any harness where post-hoc audit or CI must grade path not just answer.
- *Overkill/risk:* Requires instrumentation discipline (version-stable span names, message shape). pydantic-ai mitigates by duplicating constants (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:65-89`); agent-framework documents text-only `query`/`response` loss for multi-modal (`docs/decisions/0023-foundry-evals-integration.md:555-557`).

**P2. Deterministic tool-choice scoring with partial credit.**
- *Problem:* Exact-order boolean checks are brittle (harmless reordering fails); LLM judges are non-deterministic and expensive.
- *Demonstrated by:* pydantic-ai `TrajectoryMatch` with `exact`/`in_order` (LCS F1)/`any_order` (multiset F1) and reason strings (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:250-366`); pydantic-ai `ToolCorrectness` Counter multiset with `allow_extra`/`include_failed` (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:189-244`); agent-framework `tool_calls_present` unordered extras-OK + `tool_call_args_match` subset matching (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:1173-1277`); contrast with langgraph notebook `check_trajectory_custom` exact list equality (`examples/rag/langgraph_crag_local.ipynb:724-745`) and crewai `ToolSelectionEvaluator` LLM prompt ("DO NOT evaluate how many times each tool was used", `lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/metrics/tools_metrics.py:79`).
- *Why it works:* F1/LCS tolerates extra/missing tools while still penalizing wrong order; Counter respects duplicate necessity; subset arg matching tolerates verbosity.
- *When to copy:* Tool-heavy agents where the minimal valid set is known or derivable. *Risk:* LCS can over-score with small alphabet repeats (`['search','search','format','format']` F1=0.667, `tests/evals/test_agentic_evaluators.py:518-540`).
- *Evidence:* pydantic-ai ~1k-line fixture suite pinning LCS/any_order hand-calculations (`tests/evals/test_agentic_evaluators.py:1-1032`); agent-framework `test_local_eval.py:543-630` extras/mode/args-mismatch tests.

**P3. Tiered evaluator extensibility (deterministic + LLM-judge + DSL).**
- *Problem:* No single evaluator family covers syntactic sequence, semantic argument correctness, and open-ended output quality.
- *Demonstrated by:* pydantic-ai splits agentic deterministic (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py`) from rubric (`LLMJudge`/`GEval` at `pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/common.py:224-339`) and adds `HasMatchingSpan(SpanQuery)` (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/common.py:341-355`, `pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/otel/span_tree.py:29-404`) as rule-engine DSL; agent-framework `@evaluator` param injection for sync/async functions returning `bool`/`float`/`dict`/`CheckResult` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:1296-1351,1354`) plus Foundry cloud suite including `tool_selection`/`tool_call_accuracy` (`python/packages/foundry/agent_framework_foundry/_foundry_evals.py:107-126`); crewai `BaseEvaluator` plugin with `MetricCategory` discriminator (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/base_evaluator.py:20-69`).
- *Why it works:* Evaluators compose in one `Dataset.evaluate()`/`AgentEvaluator.evaluate()` run with independent scores, so trajectory and answer quality remain orthogonal (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/dataset.py:1152-1163`).

**P4. Event-bus / OTel span capture as trajectory source.**
- *Problem:* Instrumenting executors directly couples evaluation to execution.
- *Demonstrated by:* crewai `EvaluationTraceCallback` singleton on `ToolUsage*Event`/`LLMCall*Event` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/evaluation_listener.py:58-146`); pydantic-ai `CURRENT_TASK_RUN` ContextVar + `context_subtree` (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/_task_run.py:35-77`); letta `trace_method` + `TelemetryManager` dual-write (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/letta/letta/otel/tracing.py:228-435`); langgraph `_TimedAttemptScope`/`_AttemptEvent` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_retry.py:87-415`).
- *When to copy:* When you need evaluators decoupled from loop internals. *Caution:* crewai's singleton leaks across tests and misses mid-trajectory data via `tool_uses[:5]` sampling (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/metrics/tools_metrics.py:189,349`) and `ReasoningEfficiencyEvaluator` 6-call heuristic (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/metrics/reasoning_metrics.py:386-398`).

**P5. Graceful degradation on missing telemetry (none-vs-zero).**
- *Problem:* Evaluators run in varied runtime/privacy modes (Logfire disabled, `include_content=False`, observability sampled).
- *Demonstrated by:* pydantic-ai every agentic evaluator returns `EvaluationReason(value=False/0.0, reason=_NO_SPAN_TREE_REASON)` on `SpanTreeRecordingError` (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:182,222-226,324-328`), `ArgumentCorrectness` explicit "include_content may be disabled" failure (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:446-453`); crewai evaluators return `EvaluationScore(score=None)` on empty `llm_calls`/`tool_uses` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/metrics/reasoning_metrics.py:61-65`, `tools_metrics.py:44-51`) then aggregation silently excludes `None` — the anti-pattern version of the same idea.
- *Why it works:* Keeps dataset runs green while surfacing failures as scores, not crashes.

**P6. Report-level composite scoring and display.**
- *Problem:* A trajectory yields multiple scores (tool F1, arg subset, side-effect count, LLM rubric); consumers need per-case rows and aggregates.
- *Demonstrated by:* pydantic-ai `ReportCase`/`ReportCaseAggregate.average()` averaging scores/labels/assertions (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/reporting/__init__.py:86-392`); agent-framework `EvalScoreResult`/`EvalItemResult`/`EvalResults` with `result_counts`/`per_evaluator`/`sub_results`/`report_url` and `assert_score_at_least` helpers (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:304-642`); crewai `AgentEvaluationResult`/`AgentAggregatedEvaluationResult` with `AggregationStrategy` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/experimental/evaluation/base_evaluator.py:72-106`); langfuse `aggregateScores` per-run averages (`web/src/features/experiments/server/router.ts:418-428`).

## Key Differences

**Where the trajectory record lives — and therefore what scoring can see.**

| Aspect | agent-framework | pydantic-ai | crewai | openhands | langgraph | langfuse | letta/openai |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|--------|-----------|-----------|----------|--------------|
| Record | `conversation` on `EvalItem` | `SpanTree` via OTel | `traces[agent_id_task_id]` dict `{tool_uses, llm_calls}` | `Sequence[LLMConvertibleEvent]` plus `git_patch` (currently `None` live) | `Checkpoints` + `TaskPayload`s + stream events | One `ObservationForEval` per job or one flat trace prompt | `Step` rows / span hierarchy (no eval read path) |
| Ordering | Caller-chosen splitter / `per_turn_items` cumulative | `start_timestamp` sorted (`agentic.py:165-169`) | Insertion-order trace dict, sampled `[:5]` | Prefix whole-history rescore | Paginated `get_state_history` applies `pending_writes` | Single entity only; sibling spans never joined | Ordered by `step_id`/`run_id` but never scored |
| Result | Whole-trajectory, last-turn, or per-turn — still one score per `EvalItem` | F1/bool per evaluator over ordered spans | One LLM score per metric per task | One `success` prob per prefix | No score in-repo | No trajectory score | No score |

**Tool-choice precision vs coverage.** pydantic-ai and agent-framework are deterministic and duplicate-sensitive (Counter) with `allow_extra` / unordered extras-OK; crewai uses LLM prompts that explicitly ignore count; openhands uses a single label `improper_tool_use_or_setup` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/impl/api/taxonomy.py:17`); langfuse has the telemetry (`observationForEval.ts:15-75` `tool_calls`, `tool_call_names`) but no scorer; langgraph's demo uses brittle exact-order equality (`examples/rag/langgraph_crag_local.ipynb:731`). The difference maps to cost vs reproducibility: agent-framework's `LocalEvaluator` is CI-offline free; crewai pays six LLM calls per task and openhands one per prefix.

**Context: tokenized but not graded.** letta tokenizes every section of context into `ContextWindowOverview` via three tokenizer backends (`studies/agent-harness-study/sources/letta/letta/services/context_window_calculator/context_window_calculator.py:249-384`) — the richest *measurement* in the study — yet never scores it. pydantic-ai and agent-framework expose `metrics`/`context` but ship no `ContextUtilization` evaluator (`agent-framework` gap: `context.*usage` yields no hits beyond `groundedness`; pydantic-ai: collected as `gen_ai.usage.*` at `_task_run.py:59-75` but only `MaxModelRequests` bounds turns). langfuse is the inverse: it *does* score context, but only RAG retrieval (`Contextrelevance`, `Contextcorrectness`, `Faithfulness` at `worker/src/constants/managed-evaluators.json:62-242`) — useful for grounding but not for multi-step history retention or window utilization. openai-agents-sdk tracks `cached_input_tokens`/`reasoning_tokens` (`src/agents/usage.py:195-229`) yet conflates cache savings with quality if naively scored.

**Recovery: universally implemented, never scored.** Every source has retry logic (agent-framework `WorkflowCheckpoint`, langgraph `RetryPolicy`/`NodeTimeoutError`, openai `model_retry.py:16-551`, letta `empty_response_retry_limit=3`, openhands condenser/nudge/LLM retry, crewai guardrail `guardrail_max_retries`). No source measures `recovery_rate`, `steps_to_recovery`, or "did the post-failure path become minimal?". The nearest proxies — crewai `ToolInvocationEvaluator` error_rate-to-LLM (`tools_metrics.py:320-372`), pydantic-ai `include_failed` flag (`agentic.py:22-30`), openhands `improper_tool_use_or_setup` prob threshold `0.75` triggering `should_refine` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/impl/api/critic.py:135`) — feed the signal into a judge rather than scoring the recovery event itself. langgraph's `_AttemptEvent` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_retry.py:98-126`) and `tasks[i].error` are the raw material most ready to build such a metric; they are consumed only by server infrastructure.

## Tradeoffs

**Whole-trajectory vs per-step vs prefix rescoring.**
- *Whole-trajectory* (agent-framework `FULL`, `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_evaluation.py:167`): simple, LLM-judge-friendly, but conflates multiple failures into one score and masks *where* the path diverged.
- *Per-turn expansion* (`per_turn_items()`, workflow per-agent decomposition): restores localization at turn boundaries without per-tool-call granularity; requires synthesizing N items and losing tool-result interleaving nuance.
- *Prefix rescoring* (openhands `all_actions`): achieves per-step visibility (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/agent.py:893` attaches `critic_result` to each `ActionEvent`) at linear API cost and without step-level attribution — each score is "all history through step k", not "step k in isolation".
- *Span-F1* (pydantic-ai): avoids all three extremes by scoring ordered sequences with partial credit, but counts nested/sub-agent tool calls globally unless scoped via `SpanQuery` (`pydantic_evals/pydantic_evals/evaluators/agentic.py:33-34`).

**Local determinism vs cloud/LLM fidelity.**
- Local checks (agent-framework `tool_calls_present`/`keyword_check`, pydantic-ai LCS F1) are fast, reproducible, offline — and shallow (presence only, no semantic relevance). Cloud judges (Foundry `tool_selection`/`tool_call_success`, crewai six metrics, langfuse `Faithfulness` v2 deconstructing claims) capture semantic quality but are billed, non-deterministic, and parse-fragile — every crewai metric can return `score=None` on JSON failure (`tools_metrics.py:141-146`, `reasoning_metrics.py:219-225`, `goal_metrics.py:83-88`) inflating aggregated scores via `valid_scores` exclusion. The hybrid not demonstrated in any source — deterministic sequence diff plus an LLM rationale — is the natural next step.

**Explicit trajectory schema vs generic variable mapping.**
- Opinionated schema (pydantic-ai `SpanTree`, agent-framework `EvalItem.conversation`) enables F1, ordering, and ancestor/descendant queries but couples evaluators to that framework's instrumentation. Generic mapping (langfuse `{{var}}` + `jsonSelector` at `packages/shared/src/features/evals/types.ts:88-107`) stays framework-agnostic and supports RAG/chat/tool observation equally, but cannot express `{{steps[*].tool_calls}}` and loses cross-step comparison. Each is correct for its owner — product shape dictates the choice.

**Evaluation vs actuation coupling.**
- Decoupled (agent-framework, pydantic-ai, letta/openai observability): scores report but do not mutate the run. Safe for CI but cannot drive auto-retry. Coupled (openhands `IterativeRefinementConfig` `success_threshold=0.6`/`max_iterations=3` at `_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/base.py:20` plus `response_dispatch.py:283` corrective nudge): a single `success` probability becomes a control signal. Compact but dangerous — `git_patch=None` (`critic_mixin.py:64`) renders two critics as dead code (`agent_finished.py:49` always 0.0), and threshold misconfiguration (0.0 disables retry, 1.0 retries forever) has no guard.

**Singleton/event-bus capture vs per-run ContextVar.**
- crewai's global `EvaluationTraceCallback` singleton (`evaluation_listener.py:40-46`) simplifies wiring but risks cross-test pollution and unbounded `traces` growth. pydantic-ai's `ContextVar` `CURRENT_TASK_RUN` and per-case `context_subtree` isolate runs cleanly but require the eval harness to own the task boundary. The difference explains why crewai's `ExperimentRunner.to_json()` excludes `agent_evaluations` by default (`result.py:37`) while pydantic-ai can re-evaluate from a serialized `SpanTree`.

## Decision Guide

**If you are building a framework/library and need CI-grade trajectory regression:**
Copy Model A. Define one canonical record (messages or spans) captured outside evaluators, ship deterministic sequence scorers (`Counter` multiset with `allow_extra`, LCS F1 for order, subset argument diff), and add a generic rule DSL (`SpanQuery` / `HasMatchingSpan` or `@evaluator` injection) for one-offs. Example seams: pydantic-ai `agentic.py:65-89` dual-version constants for instrumentation stability and agent-framework `ConversationSplitter` (`_evaluation.py:76-104`) for factoring. Do not start with LLM judges — add `LLMJudge`/`GEval` as an orthogonal family once deterministic baselines are stable.

**If you are building an application and need "good path" insight quickly:**
Adopt Model B but harden it. Isolate the trace buffer per run (replace crewai's singleton with scoped instance), bound sampling (replace `tool_uses[:5]` with time-weighted coverage), and add deterministic tool precision/recall beside the LLM judges so a judge outage does not blind you. Budget 6+ LLM calls per trajectory for crewai's default; for openhands, enable `all_actions` only on a sample — default `finish_and_message` evaluates only terminals.

**If you are a graph or transport layer (langgraph-like):**
Do what langgraph does but close the loop. Keep scoring out of core to remain provider-agnostic, but ship a reference `evaluation/` package (`BaseTrajectoryEvaluator` + `ToolCallOrderEvaluator(edit_distance_threshold)`, `ContextFaithfulnessEvaluator`, `RecoveryEvaluator` over `StateSnapshot`/`CheckpointPayload`) so users have an offline pytest path without LangSmith. Promote `_AttemptEvent` (`_retry.py:98-126`) to a public telemetry API and surface `recovered_after_attempts` in `CheckpointPayload.tasks[*]` — the raw material for any recovery metric.

**If you are a platform (langfuse-like):**
Decide *before* promising trajectory evaluation. Ingestion-time single-entity scoring is cheap and idempotent (deterministic `buildDeterministicEvalScoreIds` at `evalScoreIds.ts:6-38`); trajectory scoring requires a `Trajectory` evaluator type with ordered `ObservationForEval[]` payload and array-expansion mapping (`{{steps[*].tool_calls}}`), plus a `trajectory_scores` table for `groupBy trajectory` rollups. Without that, per-observation isolation is a feature — don't paper over it with prompt tricks that penalize good answers with bad reasoning invisibly.

**If you have telemetry but no scoring (letta/openai-like):**
Resist adding an LLM judge first. Add two deterministic, sidecar-table evaluators: *tool-choice parsimony* (tool F1 vs minimal set derived from task or annotation) and *context-efficiency* (tokens per goal progress from `ContextWindowOverview`/`Usage` deltas). The existing checkpointed `StepMetrics` (`letta_agent_v2.py:941-1034`) and span hierarchies (`openai-agents-sdk: trace()->task->agent->turn` at `tracing/create.py:124-350`) are the correct insertion points; persist scores as `StepEvaluation` keyed by `step_id` or `SpanEvaluation` keyed by span id, not as extra latency columns.

## Practical Tips

- **Capture the trajectory at the boundary, not inside each evaluator.** pydantic-ai's `context_subtree`/`TaskRun` (`dataset.py:970-988`) shows the pattern: one capture, many evaluators, plus an online path (`online.py:496-720`) that mirrors it via `OnlineEvalConfig`. crewai's singleton shows the failure mode — global mutable trace, missing knowledge-retrieval events despite docstring mentioning them (`evaluation_listener.py:30-36`).
- **Sort by time, not insertion order.** pydantic-ai `_extract_tool_calls` sorts by `start_timestamp` before F1 (`agentic.py:165-169`); agent-framework `per_turn_items` uses cumulative context order. Without explicit sorting, nested/sub-agent interleaving produces non-deterministic scores.
- **Design for `include_content=False`.** `ArgumentCorrectness` failing with a diagnostic when JSON args are unavailable (`agentic.py:446-453`) is preferable to silently scoring 0. Propagate the cause into `reason` so dashboards distinguish "wrong args" from "privacy mode".
- **Count nested/sub-agent calls explicitly.** pydantic-ai documents "including tool calls made by nested sub-agents" (`agentic.py:33-34`) — decide before you aggregate, otherwise parent tool parsimony metrics inflate.
- **Filter output functions and deferred calls before scoring.** `running output function:` spans and `ApprovalRequired`/`CallDeferred` spans (`agentic.py:71-81,105-136`) must not count toward `ToolCorrectness`; verify with dedicated tests (`tests/evals/test_agentic_evaluators.py:211-252`).
- **Make evaluators independently runnable and comparable.** pydantic-ai returns `EvaluationScalar | EvaluationReason` per evaluator (`evaluator.py:27-101`) and reports map `scores/labels/assertions`; agent-framework returns `EvalResults` with `per_evaluator`/`sub_results` (`_evaluation.py:373-438`). This is how you assert that a correct trajectory can coexist with a wrong final answer.
- **Add trajectory-level assertions to CI alongside output assertions.** Agent-framework's `assert_score_at_least`/`assert_dimension_score_at_least` (`_evaluation.py:502-642`) and pydantic-ai's typed `assertions` vs `scores` separation are copyable patterns. Without them, PRs pass while silently bloating tool calls (openai `evals.py:240-302` exact-artifact checks would still pass after 4 redundant web searches).
- **Test the evaluator's failure envelope, not just the happy path.** Include: no-span-tree degradation (pydantic-ai), empty `llm_calls`/`tool_uses` returning `None` (crewai `reasoning_metrics.py:61-65`), JSON parse failures, `toolChoice` resetting (`tests/test_tool_choice_reset.py:11-48`), and provider-native tool blind spots — all of which produce systematic false passes if untested.
- **Version-pin or duplicate instrumentation keys.** Both pydantic-ai and agent-framework duplicate tool span keys to survive v2/v3 drift; langgraph's debug payload (`debug.py:41-279`) and openai-agents-sdk's `function_schema` show the same lesson — evaluator-grade contracts on span/message fields need pinned tests, not just tracer decoration.
- **Bound history.** `get_state_history` without `limit` on 100-step graphs (`test_invoke_many_processes`) and openhands prefix rescoring linearly with length both grow unbounded; add `limit`/`before` pagination and trajectory export truncation guards (langfuse 5.5MB payload cap at `codeEvalDispatcherTypes.ts:5`).

## Anti-Patterns / Caution Signs

- **Dead scaffolding that suggests trajectory evaluation exists.** crewai `LLM.completion_cost: float | None` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/llm.py:370`) and openhands `EmptyPatchCritic`/`AgentFinishedCritic` wired with `git_patch=None` (`critic_mixin.py:64`, `agent_finished.py:49`) both promise scoring that is never exercised — grep for consumers, not just definitions.
- **Global singleton trace buffers.** crewai `EvaluationTraceCallback` singleton (`evaluation_listener.py:40-46`) plus `_state_lock` in `AgentEvaluator` (`agent_evaluator.py:61`) leak across tests and grow unbounded; long-lived processes conflate sibling runs. Caution sign: trajectory capture keyed on ambient `current_agent_id`/`current_task_id` rather than explicit run handles.
- **Silent `None` → exclusion inflation.** crewai metrics return `score=None` on parse failure and aggregation does `valid_scores` filtering (`evaluation_display.py:250-305`); openhands's `_NO_SPAN_TREE_REASON` vs crewai's `None` show opposite treatments — the latter inflates overall scores by discarding failures. Alert when aggregate rises as parse errors increase.
- **Sampling that hides late errors.** crewai `tool_uses[:5]` (`tools_metrics.py:189,349`) and reasoning 6-call heuristic (`reasoning_metrics.py:386-398`) truncate long trajectories; late loops or late recovery never reach a judge.
- **Holistic score hiding step quality.** openhands `success` prob threshold `0.5` (`result.py:7`) and display threshold `0.2` (`taxonomy.py:82`) vs refinement threshold `0.75` (`critic.py:48`) decouple "what is shown" from "what triggers control" — `improper_tool_use_or_setup=0.18` is suppressed yet diagnostic. Signal suppressed at display time is unavailable for retrospective analysis.
- **Retry masking.** langgraph `RetryPolicy` retries on `NodeTimeoutError` by default (`tests/test_retry.py:232-244`) then clears writes, leaving `tasks[i].error == None` in the final checkpoint; openai-agents-sdk `apply_retry_attempt_usage` folds retried token counts; letta summarization loops recover from `context_window_overflow` (`agent.py:957-989`) — all erase evidence that the path was initially poor unless evaluators inspect `_AttemptEvent` history or per-attempt usage deltas.
- **Filter-only routing masquerading as sequence evaluation.** langfuse filter columns (`observationForEval.ts:332-346` `toolCalls`, `calledToolNames`) support `tool_call_count > 0` but not "tool A before observation B" — a recovered-after-failure trajectory is indistinguishable from one that never failed at the filter level.
- **Sensitive-data gating blinds deep evaluation.** openai-agents-sdk `trace_include_sensitive_data=False` (common under ZDR, `docs/tracing.md:132-140`) withholds `generation_span` inputs/outputs; letta `trace_method` truncates at 2MB per param / 4MB total and excludes `messages`/`memory` (`otel/tracing.py:252-269`). A post-hoc scorer without those fields cannot grade prompt or context use.
- **Experimental signals with no CI gate.** crewai trajectory metrics live under `experimental` and `Crew.test()` never runs them; langgraph's `check_trajectory_*` lives only in notebooks; openhands has no backend `tests/unit` for `CriticMixin` (`frontend/__tests__/components/v1/chat/event-message-components/critic-result-display.test.tsx:5` is display-only). Brittleness correlates with absence from the tested path.

## Notable Absences

Across all eight sources, checked against each report's explicit search evidence and file boundaries:

- **Context-window utilization / retrieval-precision evaluator:** absent everywhere. No evaluator measures history-window retention, truncation strategy effectiveness, or tool-output-to-next-step grounding. The richest measurement (letta `ContextWindowOverview` + `ContextWindowCalculator`) and the richest scoring (langfuse `Faithfulness` v2 deconstructing claims at `managed-evaluators.json:229-242`) address orthogonal slices — volume vs RAG grounding — but neither answers "was the right step's output carried forward?".
- **Recovery-from-failure evaluation:** absent everywhere. No dataset item tagged `requires_recovery: true`, no `RecoverySuccess`/`ErrorRecoverySteps` evaluator, no fault-injection harness measuring `steps_to_recovery` or `recovery_rate`. The four sources with resilience features (langgraph `RetryPolicy`, openai `model_retry.py`, letta `empty_response_retry_limit`, openhands iterative refinement) all lack a metric over them.
- **Step-indexed trajectory scorer:** absent everywhere. No first-class `list[StepScore]` (step index, `tool_call_id`, `passed`, `reason`) per trajectory. Agent-framework's `EvalItemResult.error_code` + `token_usage` and openhands' `ActionEvent.critic_result` / `MessageEvent.critic_result` are per-item but not per-step *within* an item; pydantic-ai's agentic evaluators score the *sequence* as one F1.
- **Tool-choice relevance evaluator beyond membership/args:** absent everywhere. No evaluator answers "among plausible tools, was this the *best*?" — all deterministic scorers check membership/order/args against a minimal set; LLM judges check relevance qualitatively but sans ground-truth tool ranking.
- **Aggregated trajectory health score:** absent as persistent, queryable concept. `EvalResults.sub_results` and `ReportCaseAverage` aggregate numerically, but no `TrajectoryScore` table or `GET /v1/runs/{run_id}/trajectory/score` endpoint exists; langfuse's scores link individually to `traceId`/`observationId` (`evalScoreEvent.ts:16-61`) with no trajectory rollup.
- **Bounded, spec-consistent tracing contract for external evaluators:** absent. LangGraph and openai-agents-sdk stream traces optimized for observation, not for stable evaluation schema — field renames can silently break external trajectory dashboards with no contract test tying `GenerationSpanData.export()` or `CheckpointPayload` shapes to an external eval schema.

If trajectory evaluation exists outside the studied snapshot (e.g., langfuse `ee/`, OpenHands `benchmarks` external repo at `Development.md:330`, LangGraph Platform server-side `_AttemptEvent` consumer), it was not in scope per isolation rules — but absence from the OSS harness matters for adopters building CI.

## Per-Source Notes

- **agent-framework (6):** The most protocol-complete in-framework evaluation layer. `EvalItem` + `ConversationSplitter` + `@evaluator` param injection (`_evaluation.py:1296-1351`) plus workflow helpers (`_extract_agent_eval_data` at `_evaluation.py:941-1009`, `evaluate_workflow` at `1833-2021`) and Foundry dual path (`_foundry_evals.py:107-126,805-816`) are well-documented and tested. Gap is step granularity inside one `EvalItem` and missing context/recovery metrics. Signal of `experimental` feature flag (`_evaluation.py:68`) and split-mismatch silent semantics counsel caution.

- **pydantic-ai (6):** The most deterministic, test-backed trajectory suite. `ToolCorrectness`/`TrajectoryMatch`/`ArgumentCorrectness` with `SpanQuery` DSL and explicit version handling are copyable patterns; ~1k-line fixtures and invariant-ordering tests are the study's strongest evidence of durability. Gaps — non-local provider tools invisible (`agentic.py:16-19`), deferred calls never counted (`agentic.py:83-85`), nested sub-agent calls counted globally — are explicitly documented, not hidden.

- **crewai (5):** The only source with an installed end-to-end trajectory runner (`ExperimentRunner` at `experiment/runner.py:26-165` + `ExperimentResults.compare_with_baseline` at `experiment/result.py:48-143`). Strength is breadth over six `MetricCategory` dimensions; weakness is LLM-only scoring (six calls + optional synthesis at `evaluation_display.py:306-378`), sampling fragility, and complete isolation from the production `Crew.test()` path.

- **openhands (5):** Best integration of scoring with actuation (iterative refinement `success_threshold=0.6` / `max_iterations=3` at `critic/base.py:20` and corrective nudge at `response_dispatch.py:283`) and per-event persistence (`action.py:69` `critic_result`). Its lesson is negative: a single `success` probability over prefix history is the ceiling for holistic classifiers — deterministic per-step and per-dimension evaluators are needed to answer "which step failed?".

- **langgraph (4):** Shares top marks for trajectory *observability* (stream modes, checkpoints, attempt events, time-travel). Its notebook `check_trajectory_react`/`check_trajectory_custom` pattern (`examples/rag/langgraph_crag_local.ipynb:701-812`) establishes the common anti-pattern to avoid — exact-order boolean matching on tool names with no arg or semantic check — and delegates what should be in-library (`BaseTrajectoryEvaluator` consuming `StateSnapshot`) to SaaS.

- **langfuse (3):** The study's cleanest evaluation *platform* and poorest trajectory system. Single-entity LLM-as-judge + sandboxed code evaluators (`codeEvalExecution.ts:105-128`) with declarative variable mapping plus five managed RAG context evaluators are excellent for RAG grounding but, by choosing per-observation isolation and flat prompt substitution, make trajectory reasoning deliberately impossible to express.

- **letta (2):** Instrumentation-first architecture with negligible evaluation. `Step`/`StepStatus`/`StepMetrics`/`ProviderTrace`/`ContextWindowOverview`/`MetricRegistry` (20+ OTel instruments at `otel/metric_registry.py:13-503`) provide near-lossless trajectory capture at performance-telemetry granularity — latency, timing, token breakdown — with zero judgment. `feedback: positive|negative` on `Step` (`schemas/step.py:57-58`) is the lone quality signal: binary, human-labeled, unaggregated.

- **openai-agents-sdk (2):** Mirrors letta intentionally — observability over evaluation. Span hierarchy, `TurnSpanData`/`TaskSpanData` per-turn usage, `RunResult.new_items`/`input_guardrail_results`, and lifecycle hooks (`src/agents/lifecycle.py:13-103`) are an excellent skeleton for a trajectory scorer, but no scorer is attached; tutorial `evals.py:240-302` asserts only final-artifact equality, and only `EvaluationFeedback(score: Literal["pass","needs_improvement","fail"])` in the LLM-as-judge demo (`examples/agent_patterns/llm_as_a_judge.py:25-71`) resembles a trajectory score.
