## Executive Summary

No source in this study can answer "who is responsible for this action?" as an organizational question. All seven frameworks converge on the same delegation model: the **builder/operator/organization is responsible**, with the library disclaiming liability (`agent-framework` `README.md:207`, `openai-agents-sdk` `LICENSE:15`, `openhands` `LICENSE:28`, `letta` `TERMS.md:14`). Technical attribution is fragmented but consistently implemented for two axes — *what tool ran* and *was a human gate hit* — while the remaining three questions are universally weak: no framework injects a model-output disclaimer or watermark, no framework binds a principal identity to an approval, and no framework ships a formal accountability document or RACI.

Scores cluster at **4–5/10 (present but inconsistent, weakly documented, fragile)**. The three 5s (`agent-framework`, `crewai`, `langgraph` at `agent-framework.md:20`, `crewai.md:20`, `langgraph.md:20`) earn a point over the four 4s by providing either opt-in session/checkpoint durability or ubiquitous event fingerprinting. The 4s (`letta`, `openai-agents-sdk`, `openhands`, `pydantic-ai` at `letta.md:20`, `openai-agents-sdk.md:20`, `openhands.md:20`, `pydantic-ai.md:20`) have equally mature tool/human primitives but even weaker organizational binding (nullable identities, tracing-as-optional-audit, client-forgable approvals).

The system can answer **what/when/which-tool** via `tool_call_id` + `tool_name` + checkpoint/event linkage (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:338`, `langgraph` `libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/tool_node.py:789`, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:2165`, `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tools/tool_usage.py:273`). It cannot reliably answer **who** (principal), **on whose authority**, or **is this text AI-generated** without external scaffolding the deployer must build.

## Core Thesis

Responsibility in current agent harnesses is **delegated, not designed**. The frameworks treat accountability as *provenance plumbing* — message `author_name`, `tool_call_id`, `run_id`/`execution_uuid`, `Interrupt`/`DeferredToolRequests`, `ToolOrigin`, `source` — rather than as *governance*. This yields a durable audit of execution steps but leaves organizational accountability (model vs runtime vs tool author vs user vs operator vs organization) implicit, opt-in, and erasable.

Two structural forces explain the convergence and divergence:

*Convergence despite different stacks:* Every framework must solve the same LLM → tool-call → human-gate loop, so each independently invents `call_id`-keyed attribution and a suspend/resume approval handshake. The mechanism is isomorphic whether spelled `function_approval_request` (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:1212`), `ApprovalRequestMessage` (`letta` `letta/schemas/letta_message.py:306`), `ToolApprovalItem` (`openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/items.py:556`), `DeferredToolRequests` (`pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:27`), `HumanFeedbackRequestedEvent` (`crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/events/types/flow_events.py:244`), `GraphInterrupt` (`langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:534`), or `WAITING_FOR_CONFIRMATION` + `UserRejectObservation` (`openhands` `_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:52` + `_sdk_inspect/sdk/event/llm_convertible/observation.py:71`).

*Divergence by product shape:* Library-first harnesses (`langgraph`, `pydantic-ai`, `openai-agents-sdk`, `agent-framework`) push persistence and identity to the deployer (checkpoint saver, OTel exporter, `AgentSession`, `RunState` JSON). Multi-tenant or service-oriented harnesses (`letta`, `openhands`) add ORM tables or `AppConversationInfo.created_by_user_id` (`letta` `letta/orm/message.py:59`, `openhands` `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:115`) but still allow nullable principals (`letta` tests show `sender_id: null` at `tests/test_agent_files/test_agent.af:206`, `openhands` `ADMIN = SpecifyUserContext(user_id=None)` at `openhands/app_server/user/specifiy_user_context.py:67`). Orchestration-first harnesses (`crewai`) add fleet-level correlation (`execution_uuid` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/execution.py:22`, `Fingerprint` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/security/fingerprint.py:42`) and event-graph edges (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/event_record.py:52`).

The practical consequence is a **provenance-rich, accountability-poor** architecture: an operator with event logs can reconstruct *which agent/task/tool produced which output at which checkpoint*, but cannot prove *which human authorized it* or *that the output is AI-generated* without adding identity binding, disclaimers, and retention policy the framework deliberately omits.

## Approach Models

Three approach models emerge; most sources hybridize the first two and optionally layer the third.

### Model A — Delegation-to-Builder via Typed Handshake ("You own accountability")

*Vocabulary:* responsibility assigned to **user/operator/organization**; runtime provides *how* to record decisions, not *who* is liable.

*Mechanism:* A typed request/response pair reifies human consent as a checkpointable continuation, not a blocking callback. Tool execution is denied or deferred until the caller re-invokes with approvals.

*Exemplars:* `agent-framework` (`Content.from_function_approval_request` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:1212` + `ToolApprovalMiddleware` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:371`), `pydantic-ai` (`DeferredToolRequests`/`DeferredToolResults` at `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:27` + `ToolApproved`/`ToolDenied` at `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:103`), `openai-agents-sdk` (`ToolApprovalItem` at `src/agents/items.py:556` + `RunState.approve`/`reject` at `src/agents/run_state.py:1285` + `NextStepInterruption` at `src/agents/run_internal/run_steps.py:22`), `langgraph` (`Interrupt` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:534` + `Command(resume=...)` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:759` + `interrupt_before`/`after` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/graph/state.py:1170`), `letta` (`RequiresApprovalToolRule` at `letta/schemas/tool_rule.py:348` + `ApprovalRequestMessage` at `letta/schemas/letta_message.py:306`), `openhands` (`WAITING_FOR_CONFIRMATION` at `_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:52` + `UserRejectObservation` at `_sdk_inspect/sdk/event/llm_convertible/observation.py:71`).

*Best fit:* Provider-agnostic libraries and BYO-persistence deployments where the operator must integrate auth.

*Limitation:* The handshake records *what was approved for which call_id*, not *who approved, when, or under which policy* — principal, timestamp, and signature are absent in every source.

### Model B — Event/Checkpoint Graph as Forensic Ledger ("What, when, which step")

*Vocabulary:* attribution to **runtime/agent/task/tool** execution identity, not to organizational principal.

*Mechanism:* Every state transition writes a checkpoint or event node with correlation IDs. History APIs reconstruct lineage.

*Exemplars:* `langgraph` (`CheckpointMetadata.source: "input"|"loop"|"update"|"fork"` at `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:38` + `PregelLoop._put_checkpoint` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:1016` + `get_state_history` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1479`), `crewai` (`BaseEvent` fields `source_fingerprint`/`source_type`/`agent_id`/`task_id` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/events/base_events.py:66` + `EventRecord` graph edges at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/event_record.py:52` + `execution_uuid` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/execution.py:22`), `agent-framework` (`author_name` propagation at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_agents.py:1065` + workflow `executor_id` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_agent.py:505`), `pydantic-ai` (`run_id`/`conversation_id` on `ModelRequest` at `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1852` + `GraphAgentState.run_id` at `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_agent_graph.py:307`), `letta` (`Message`/`Step`/`Run` ORM rows at `letta/orm/message.py:23`/`letta/orm/step.py:20`/`letta/orm/run.py:22`), `openhands` (`EventLog` dir `EVENTS_DIR` at `_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:250` + `EventService.search_events` at `openhands/app_server/event/event_service.py:22`).

*Best fit:* Debuggability, time-travel, replay, and tracing-first observability.

*Limitation:* Retention is unbounded by default (`langgraph` `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:176` no TTL, note at `THREAT_MODEL.md:DC3`), durability is caller-dependent (`agent-framework` `AgentSession` required at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:373`, `crewai` in-memory `EventRecord` without listener, `openhands` `InMemoryFileStore` fallback at `_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:323`), and principal is missing even when execution identity is rich.

### Model C — Actor-Gated Persistence ("Which tenant/user row owns this")

*Vocabulary:* responsibility filtered by **organization/user actor** for multi-tenancy, closest to organizational accountability but still coarse.

*Mechanism:* Every write requires an `actor: PydanticUser` or `created_by_user_id` scoping to `organization_id`.

*Exemplars:* `letta` (`actor` param at `letta/agent.py:178` + `letta/services/step_manager.py:44` + `OrmMetadataBase.created_by_id` at `letta/schemas/letta_base.py:100`), `openhands` (`AppConversationInfo.created_by_user_id` at `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:115` + `UserContext.get_user_id` at `openhands/app_server/user/user_context.py:23` + `AppConversationStartTask` at `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:316`), `crewai` (`Fingerprint` on `SecurityConfig` at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/security/security_config.py:39` + `execution_uuid` ContextVar at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/execution.py:22` hinting at Enterprise session ownership).

*Best fit:* SaaS deployments needing tenant isolation and per-org billing/audit.

*Limitation:* Identities are nullable or forgeable (`letta` `sender_id: null` common, `openhands` anonymous `ADMIN` at `openhands/app_server/user/specifiy_user_context.py:67`, `crewai` `Fingerprint.generate(seed)` without signing at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/security/fingerprint.py:54`). Scoping is access control, not an accountability invariant (no `approved_by_user_id`, no non-repudiation).

No source implements a **Dedicated Accountability Ledger Model** (append-only, tamper-evident, queryable `GET /accountability` view with RACI per action class). The gap between Model B (what happened) and Model C (which tenant) is where organizational accountability would live.

## Pattern Catalog

### 1. Suspend/Resume Approval Handshake as Typed Continuation

*Problem:* LLM tool-calls needing human consent cannot block the event loop or require a callback registry.

*Sources demonstrating it:* `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:1212`, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:27`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_context.py:57` + `src/agents/run_state.py:1285`, `langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:534` + `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:759`, `letta` `letta/schemas/letta_message.py:306`, `openhands` `_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:52`, `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/human_feedback.py:118`.

*Why it works:* The agent returns a typed request object, the runtime persists it via checkpoint/session/event store, and the caller re-invokes with a matching response. Pause/resume is composable across remote A2A, service-managed threads, and streaming (`agent-framework` `docs/decisions/0006-userapproval.md:384` suspend/resume loop, `pydantic-ai` stream `DeferredToolRequestsEvent` at `docs/deferred-tools.md:489`).

*When to copy:* Any tool with side effects where you need HITL without coupling to a UI. Align with `_approval_mode`/`requires_approval`/`needs_approval` flags already present (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_tools.py:301`, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/tools.py:306`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tool.py:486`).

*When it is risky:* Without principal + timestamp + signature, the handshake proves *a* decision, not *whose* decision. Client-supplied history models (`pydantic-ai` `docs/deferred-tools.md:101` warning, `agent-framework` `docs/decisions/0006-userapproval.md:92` service-managed amnesia) allow forgery if the operator does not re-authenticate.

*Evidence:* `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:371` middleware queuing, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_state.py:1330` serialization, `langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:836` `PregelLoop._first` resume mapping.

### 2. Tool Call Attribution Triple (name + call_id + origin)

*Problem:* Auditing which tool ran with which args by which agent, especially with MCP/hosted tools and parallel dispatch.

*Sources:* `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:2165` `BaseToolCallPart.tool_name`/`tool_call_id` + `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_instrumentation.py:346` OTel annotation, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tool.py:285` `ToolOriginType` + `src/agents/items.py:382` `ToolCallItem`, `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:338` `Content.from_function_call` + `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:61` `server_label`, `langgraph` `libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/tool_node.py:789` `ToolNode.tools_by_name` + `tool_call_id` linkage, `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tools/tool_usage.py:273` `ToolUsageStartedEvent`, `letta` `letta/orm/message.py:46` `tool_calls` + `letta/schemas/tool.py:67` `created_by_id`.

*Why it works:* `tool_call_id` mirrors the LLM `tool_calls[i].id` and echoes on `ToolReturnPart` (`pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1303`), giving exact correlation even with parallel fan-out. `ToolOrigin`/`server_label` disambiguates `FUNCTION` vs `MCP` vs `AGENT_AS_TOOL`.

*When to copy:* Always. Add `qualified_name` (`openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/_tool_identity.py:129`) or `server_label` scoping to prevent confused-deputy approvals (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:290`).

*When overkill:* Flat `tool.name` is sufficient for single-tool prototypes; qualified identity pays off when you add MCP servers or agent-as-tool composition.

*Evidence:* `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/_tool_identity.py:597` lookup map, `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/observability.py:207` OTel spans, `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tools/tool_usage.py:1089` fingerprint config injection.

### 3. Fingerprint / Run Correlation IDs

*Problem:* Correlating nested kickoffs, streamed chunks, and tool calls across workers.

*Sources:* `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/security/fingerprint.py:42` + `lib/crewai/src/crewai/execution.py:22` `execution_uuid`, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1852` `run_id`/`conversation_id` + `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_instrumentation.py:35` OTel baggage, `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:1731` `author_name`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tracing/spans.py:289` `TraceState`, `langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/runtime.py:27` `ExecutionInfo`, `letta` `letta/orm/step.py:28` `Step.trace_id`/`run_id`.

*Why it works:* ContextVar or message-carried IDs propagate without threading args; `run_id` (ephemeral) vs `conversation_id` (multi-run) distinction (`pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_agent_graph.py:307`) enables both per-turn and per-session audit.

*When to copy:* Multi-agent or multi-run sessions where one `kickoff` fans out.

*When risky:* Bare UUIDs without signing (`crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/security/security_config.py:25` TODO) provide correlation, not non-repudiation. Do not use as authorization.

*Evidence:* `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/events/base_events.py:66` stamping, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_instrumentation.py:123` `get_agent_run_baggage_attributes`.

### 4. OTel as Accountability Surrogate

*Problem:* Getting queryable "who/what called which tool" without a dedicated audit store.

*Sources:* `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/observability.py:207` + `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_tools.py:717` spans, `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/events/listeners/tracing/trace_listener.py:408`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tracing/span_data.py:28` `AgentSpanData`/`GenerationSpanData`/`FunctionSpanData`, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_instrumentation.py:308` GenAI semconv, `letta` `letta/log_context.py:24` + `letta/otel/tracing.py` spans, `openhands` `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/live_status_app_conversation_service.py:391` Laminar tracing.

*Why it works:* OTel GenAI conventions (`gen_ai.tool.name`, `gen_ai.agent.name`, `gen_ai.usage.*`) are standard and composable.

*When to copy:* As a secondary, queryable layer alongside checkpoint durability.

*When risky:* Tracing is sampling-configurable and opt-out (`openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tracing/traces.py:407` NoOp, `agent-framework` `observability.py:798` sticky disable, `pydantic-ai` no audit sink). If tracing is the only ledger, disabling or sampling = silent audit loss.

*Evidence:* `openai-agents-sdk` `docs/tracing.md:7` disable flag, `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_mcp.py:22` propagator injection.

### 5. Structured Tool Failure, Not String Parsing

*Problem:* "Tool ran but logically failed" (HTTP 200 with `ok:false`, MCP `isError`) is otherwise invisible to audit.

*Sources:* `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tools/tool_failure.py:35` `ToolFailure`/`ToolFailureRecord`/`ToolFailurePolicy` (most mature), `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_tools.py:717` `error.type` on spans, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1336` `ToolReturnPart.outcome='denied'|'failed'`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tool.py:1964` `maybe_invoke_function_tool_failure_error_function`, `langgraph` `libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/tool_node.py:922` `ToolMessage(status="error")`.

*Why it works:* Explicit outcome enums prevent silent success masking and enable policy-aware attribution on `TaskOutput.tool_failures` (`crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tasks/task_output.py:36`).

*When to copy:* When tools wrap external APIs where semantic failure != exception. Map to WARN/RAISE policies (`crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tools/tool_failure.py:111`).

*Evidence:* `crewai` `lib/crewai/tests/tools/test_tool_usage_error_attribution.py:5` tests, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1342` semantics.

### 6. Additive Tool Disclosure + ID-Strict Approval

*Problem:* Preventing phantom approvals and cache-invalidation bugs when tool sets change mid-run.

*Sources:* `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1803` `ToolAvailabilityDeltaPart.tools_added` (additive-only per `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1795` Anthropic cache comment) + `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:70` ID-strict `build_results`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_context.py:304` `_tool_invocation_status` fingerprint + `call_id` uniqueness, `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:290` server-label scoping.

*Why it works:* Additive disclosure keeps prompt cache valid; strict `tool_call_id` membership prevents `approve_all` over-authorization.

*When to copy:* Multi-step runs where the tool catalog evolves.

*Evidence:* `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:73` ValueError on unknown IDs, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_context.py:328` ModelBehaviorError on reuse.

## Key Differences

**Library-not-service vs service-gated persistence is the primary split.** `langgraph` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:176` abstract `BaseCheckpointSaver`), `pydantic-ai` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_agent_graph.py:307` caller-supplied `message_history`), `openai-agents-sdk` (`src/agents/run_state.py:191` caller-persisted `RunState` JSON), and `agent-framework` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:373` requires `AgentSession`) all make durability caller-dependent: if you do not wire a checkpointer/session/history provider + OTel exporter, accountability evaporates after process death. In contrast, `letta` (`letta/orm/message.py:23` ORM + `letta/orm/step.py:20` + `letta/orm/run.py:22`) and `openhands` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:250` `FileStore` + `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:115` `created_by_user_id`) persist by default but still allow nullable principals, trading liveness for weaker identity guarantees. `crewai` sits between: fleet-level correlation (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/execution.py:22`) exists but defaults to in-memory `EventRecord` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/event_record.py:52`) unless a listener persists it.

**Principal binding ranges from none to nullable to forgeable.** No source binds `approved_by`. The closest are: `letta` implicit `Message.sender_id` (`letta/schemas/message.py:297` "identity id or agent id") which is nullable (`letta/orm/message.py:59`), `openhands` `AppConversationInfo.created_by_user_id: str | None` (`openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:115`) with anonymous `ADMIN` fallback (`openhands/app_server/user/specifiy_user_context.py:67`), `crewai` bare-UUID `Fingerprint` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/security/fingerprint.py:54`) with `SecurityConfig` TODOs at `lib/crewai/src/crewai/security/security_config.py:25`, and `langgraph` `Runtime.ServerInfo.user: BaseUser | None` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/runtime.py:61`) which is never persisted to `CheckpointMetadata` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:38`). `agent-framework`, `openai-agents-sdk`, and `pydantic-ai` omit principal entirely by design (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:159` no `approved_by`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_context.py:57` `_ApprovalRecord` no principal, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:168` `DeferredToolResults.approvals` keyed by `tool_call_id`).

**Tool attribution granularity diverges on host/MCP disambiguation.** `agent-framework` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:61` `server_label`) and `openai-agents-sdk` (`src/agents/tool.py:285` `ToolOriginType` + `src/agents/_tool_identity.py:129` `qualified_name`) are strongest — they prevent same-named tools on different servers from sharing approvals. `pydantic-ai` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/tools.py:605` `metadata`/`toolset_id`/`capability_id`) carries ownership hints but without scoping semantics. `crewai` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/tools/tool_usage.py:1089` `security_context` config injection) and `langgraph` (`libs/prebuilt/langgraph/prebuilt/tool_node.py:1315` `_inject_tool_args` merge) attribute at the call level but do not isolate approval policy per server. `letta` (`letta/schemas/tool.py:39` `tool_type` CUSTOM/CORE/MCP) and `openhands` (`ActionEvent.tool_call_id` at `_sdk_inspect/sdk/event/llm_convertible/action.py:22` without `tool_author`) record origin but not policy scoping.

**Model-output attribution is uniformly absent, but for different stated reasons.** `agent-framework` has no runtime disclaimer injection (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:338` `Content.type=text` without stamp, `agent-framework.md:34` grep negative). `langgraph` and `crewai` omit by library posture (`langgraph` `THREAT_MODEL.md:28` "Model output content ... out of scope", `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tasks/task_output.py:36` verbatim `TaskOutput.raw`). `openhands` substitutes a prompt instruction (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/prompts/system_prompt.j2:105` AI disclosure footer + `system_prompt.j2:41` `Co-authored-by`), unenforced. `pydantic-ai` and `openai-agents-sdk` keep provenance in spans (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1891` `TextPart` without watermark, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tracing/span_data.py:169` `GenerationSpanData` internal only, `src/agents/items.py:169` `MessageOutputItem` verbatim).

**Human decision durability ranges from schema-versioned JSON to mutable JSON column.** `openai-agents-sdk` (`src/agents/run_state.py:191` `CURRENT_SCHEMA_VERSION = "1.17"` + `src/agents/run_state.py:1330` `_serialize_approvals`) and `agent-framework` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:159` `ToolApprovalState.to_dict`/`from_dict` + `tests/workflow/test_checkpoint.py:569` checkpoint round-trip) are strongest on versioning. `pydantic-ai` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:44` `build_results` + `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1342` `outcome='denied'` in history) is durable if the caller persists `message_history`. `crewai` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/runtime/__init__.py:604` `human_feedback_history`) and `letta` (`letta/orm/custom_columns.py:116` `ApprovalsColumn` JSON) are durable per message/Flow but queryable only via deserialization. `openhands` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:52` `WAITING_FOR_CONFIRMATION` + `UserRejectObservation` at `_sdk_inspect/sdk/event/llm_convertible/observation.py:71`) is durable when `FileStore` is configured, ephemeral otherwise (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:323` InMemory fallback).

## Decision Guide

Use this rule set when designing the responsibility layer for a new harness or when hardening an existing deployment:

1. **Are you a library or a service?**
   - *Library (BYO persistence/auth):* Copy Model A (typed handshake) + Model B (checkpoint/event graph) from `langgraph` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:534` + `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:38`) or `pydantic-ai` (`pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:27`). Require the deployer to supply `checkpointer`/`message_history` sink + `Auth` handler wiring. Do not claim you provide accountability.
   - *Service/SaaS:* Copy Model C (actor-gated persistence) from `letta` (`letta/agent.py:178` `actor: PydanticUser`) or `openhands` (`openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:115` `created_by_user_id`). Enforce non-null principal at `start_app_conversation` and persist `ServerInfo.user` to checkpoint metadata — close the gap at `langgraph` `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:38` missing principal.

2. **Do you need to answer "who approved tool X?"**
   - All seven sources cannot today. If you need it, extend whichever handshake you use with `approved_by: principal_id`, `approved_at: ISO8601`, and signature (see Practical Tips). `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_context.py:57` `_ApprovalRecord` and `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:168` `DeferredToolResults.approvals` are the cleanest extension points. Do not repurpose `sender_id`/`created_by_user_id` as `approved_by` — those identify the message owner or conversation starter, not the approver (`letta` `letta/schemas/message.py:297`, `openhands` `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:115`).

3. **Do you need to prove model output is AI-generated?**
   - No source provides this. Do not rely on span metadata (`openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tracing/span_data.py:169` `GenerationSpanData`, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1928` `provider_name`) — it is tracing-internal. Add either a post-LLM `StreamTransformer` that injects a structured footer (`generated_by`, `model`, `timestamp`, `disclaimer`) before `EventLog` write (pattern suggested for `langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/stream/transformers.py:272` enrichment and `openhands` disclosure transformer) or an opt-in `output_disclaimer` flag on `TaskOutput`/`CrewOutput` (`crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tasks/task_output.py:36` future flag). Keep it opt-in to preserve composability.

4. **How many tools/servers will you integrate?**
   - Single MCP/hosted server or agent-as-tool? Use qualified identity now: `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/_tool_identity.py:129` `qualified_name` + `src/agents/_tool_identity.py:607` distinct approval buckets, or `agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:61` `server_label` scoping. Flat `tool.name` is only safe for prototypes.
   - `letta` `letta/schemas/tool.py:39` `tool_type` + `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/tools.py:605` `metadata`/`capability_id` filtering is not a substitute for server-scoped approval policy.

5. **Can you tolerate unbounded retention?**
   - If no, do not use raw `get_state_history`/`EventRecord` as compliance ledger. Add `BaseCheckpointSaver` wrapper with TTL + `export_audit_log(thread_id) → NDJSON` (as suggested for `langgraph` `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:176` and `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/provider/json_provider.py:16`) rather than consuming `list()` directly (`langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1525`). `letta` `letta/orm/message.py:23` and `openhands` `_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:250` already scope by `conversation_id`, making retention policies easier to attach.

6. **Is your audit allowed to be sampled?**
   - If no (regulated context), do not make tracing the only ledger. Persist approvals to checkpoint/session/history in addition to spans, and emit a non-samplable `custom_span("accountability.tool_approved")` that survives `trace_include_sensitive_data=False` (as proposed for `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tracing/span_data.py:135`). `agent-framework` `observability.py:798` `ENABLED` guard and `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tracing/traces.py:407` NoOp must never be the sole path.

7. **Do you need to distinguish human vs hook vs programmatic resume?**
   - Use `openhands` pattern: `UserRejectObservation.rejection_source="user"|"hook"` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/event/llm_convertible/observation.py:71`) + `ConversationState.blocked_actions` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:141`) + `langgraph` `Command` subtype tagging (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:759` — tag `command_kind: "resume"|"goto"|"update"` in checkpoint). Without the tag, `Command` is ambiguous (`langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:808` overload).

## Practical Tips

**Patterns to copy now (all maturity levels):**

- **Adopt call_id-strict approval.** Use `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:70` `build_results` exact-ID matching and `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_context.py:304` `_tool_invocation_status` fingerprinting to prevent phantom or replayed approvals. Test with duplicate-`call_id` regression (as in `crewai` `lib/crewai/tests/tools/test_tool_usage_error_attribution.py:5`).
- **Record structured tool outcomes, not just errors.** Mirror `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tools/tool_failure.py:35` `ToolFailurePolicy` and `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1342` `outcome='denied'|'failed'|'interrupted'` so callers do not parse strings to detect failure.
- **Capture per-tool-call attribution at the hook site.** Follow `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tools/tool_usage.py:273` + `1089` (fingerprint injection via `security_context` config channel) and `langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_tools.py:25` `StreamToolCallHandler` keyed by `tool_call_id`. Include `agent_id`/`task_id` (not just `agent.role` — `crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tasks/task_output.py:43` warns role-string collides).
- **Make checkpoint history queryable as audit.** Implement `get_state_history`/`StateSnapshot` projection (`langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/main.py:1391`) + `ExecutionInfo` injection (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/runtime.py:27`) + `messages` `metadata: {langgraph_node, langgraph_step, langgraph_triggers, run_id}` enrichment (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/stream/transformers.py:272`). Even if you do not use LangGraph, the shape (checkpoint `source` + `step` + `parents` + `pending_writes`) is the right minimal ledger.
- **Thread actor identity into logs/otel today.** Copy `letta` `letta/log_context.py:24` `update_log_context(agent_id, actor_id)` + `letta/log.py:153` `LogContextFilter` to bridge DB attribution to observability. It is the only pattern that joins tenant scoping to the trace span without yet requiring signed approvals.

**Patterns to delay or avoid until you have auth:**

- Do not add **standing "always approve" rules** (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:220`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_context.py:57` sticky `approved: bool`) without TTL/namespace scoping and version-marker invalidation (`openai-agents-sdk` `docs/human_in_the_loop.md:203` version marker). Stale sticky state auto-approving a newly sensitive tool is the most reported ergonomics-vs-least-privilege incident in this dimension.
- Do not use **prompt-level disclosure** (`openhands` `_sdk_inspect/sdk/agent/prompts/system_prompt.j2:105`) as a compliance control. It is a UX nicety; ship a post-LLM transformer that injects a *structured* footer (model, timestamp, principal) before `EventLog`/`Message` persistence instead.
- Do not use **bare UUID fingerprints** (`crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/security/fingerprint.py:42`) for anything beyond correlation. If you need non-repudiation, add `Fingerprint.generate(seed)` determinism plus HMAC/signing and store `principal_id` alongside.
- Do not expose **`ToolOrigin._emit_tool_origin=False`** (`openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tool.py:569`) without documenting when it is safe — suppressed origins break forensic tool-type queries.

**Caution signs the design is becoming brittle:**

- `created_by_user_id` or `sender_id` is nullable in persisted rows (`letta` `letta/orm/message.py:59`, `openhands` `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:115`, `letta` test fixture `tests/test_agent_files/test_agent.af:206`). If >5% of approvals have null principal in prod, identity is not governed.
- `get_state_history` or `EventLog` is unbounded with no TTL (`langgraph` `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:176` + `THREAT_MODEL.md:DC3`). Watch cardinality: `thread_id` × `step` × `tool_call_id` grows linearly with agent chatter.
- Tracing is the only ledger (`agent-framework` `observability.py:798` `ENABLED` gate, `openai-agents-sdk` `docs/tracing.md:7` disable, `pydantic-ai` `_instrumentation.py:35` baggage-only). If `tracing_disabled` is on in prod, you have no audit after crash.
- `InMemoryFileStore` fallback ever fires in deploy (`openhands` `_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:323` warning). That path means `BASE_STATE.json` + `EVENTS_DIR` loss on restart.
- `approval is not an authorization boundary` appears only as a doc warning (`pydantic-ai` `docs/deferred-tools.md:101`, `langgraph` `THREAT_MODEL.md:417`) with no code enforcement — expect client-forged `tool_call_id` reuse until you add server-side issuance or re-auth inside the tool body.

## Anti-Patterns / Caution Signs

- **Anonymous approval.** `_ApprovalRecord` (`openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_context.py:57`), `DeferredToolResults` (`pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/_deferred.py:168`), `RESUME` write (`langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:891`), `ApprovalReturn` (`letta` `letta/schemas/letta_message.py:31`), and `UserRejectObservation` (`openhands` `_sdk_inspect/sdk/event/llm_convertible/observation.py:71`) all lack `approved_by`/`approved_at`/`signature`. The fix is an explicit `ResponsibilitySnapshot` value object (`actor_id`/`model`/`tool_id`/`tool_author_id`/`human_approver_id`/`organization_id`/`timestamp`/`step_id`) as suggested for `letta` and `openai-agents-sdk`.

- **Ephemeral store + nullable history as audit.** `AgentSession.state` in-memory without `FileHistoryProvider`/`Cosmos`/`Redis` (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_agents.py:1201` auto-injection only when session exists), `crewai` in-memory `EventRecord` without `TraceCollectionListener`/SQLite/JSON checkpoint (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/state/provider/json_provider.py:16`), `openhands` `InMemoryFileStore` (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/conversation/state.py:323`), `openai-agents-sdk` `RunResult.new_items`/`RunState` caller-persisted JSON (`src/agents/run_state.py:191`), and `pydantic-ai` client-supplied `message_history` (`docs/deferred-tools.md:101`) all break chain-of-custody. Treat `File`/`DB` as mandatory in governed deployments; warn loudly on in-memory fallback.

- **Telemetry-only accountability with sticky disable.** `agent-framework` `observability.py:1106` `disable_instrumentation()` is sticky; `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tracing/traces.py:407` NoOp; `pydantic-ai` OTel-only. Code that logs `gen_ai.tool.name` only on spans (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_tools.py:717`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/tracing/span_data.py:135`) silently loses "who ran which tool" when tracing is off — the classic "works in dev, blank in prod" anti-pattern.

- **Service-managed thread amnesia.** `agent-framework` ADR notes `FunctionApprovalRequestContent` may not be storable on service-managed threads (`docs/decisions/0006-userapproval.md:92`); `langgraph` `ServerInfo.user` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/runtime.py:61`) is transient and not persisted. Long-term `requires_approval` flows should not target backends that drop the approval content type — log with `error.type=approval_denied` OTel event and include `approved_by` when available.

- **Standing rule / sticky approval poisoning.** `create_always_approve_tool_response` (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:220` `additional_properties.tool_approval.always_approve`) and `always_approve=True` (`openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_state.py:1285`) can be planted via crafted `function_approval_response` and then bypass future gates (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:515` `_handle_inbound_approval_response`). Guard with `ToolApprovalRule` server-label scoping and require re-approval after tool permission changes.

- **Output misattribution by omission.** Verbatim `TaskOutput.raw` (`crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tasks/task_output.py:36`, `letta` `letta/schemas/letta_message.py:364` `AssistantMessage.content`, `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/items.py:169`, `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/messages.py:1891` `TextPart`) with only role/author telemetry (`agent-framework` `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:1731` `author_name` caller-controllable) invites downstream systems to republish model text as human-authored. Enforce a post-LLM `output_disclaimer` middleware rather than relying on prompt compliance.

- **Role-string output identity.** `TaskOutput.agent: str` as role string (`crewai` `lib/crewai/src/crewai/tasks/task_output.py:43`) collides on rename and drops further for LiteAgent (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/tools/tool_usage.py:284`). Store `agent_id`/`fingerprint` alongside the role.

## Notable Absences

These gaps are consistent across all seven sources — their absence is itself the finding:

## Open Questions

- Who should sign approvals in multi-operator deployments, and how should `approved_by` / `approved_at` / `signature` be added without breaking existing `RunState`/`DeferredToolResults`/`RESUME` schemas? No source demonstrates a migration path beyond `openai-agents-sdk` `src/agents/run_state.py:196` `SCHEMA_VERSION_SUMMARIES`.
- Should output disclaimer/watermark be a harness responsibility or an application-layer policy? The library posture (`agent-framework` `TRANSPARENCY_FAQ.md:26` framework evaluation != model evaluation, `langgraph` `THREAT_MODEL.md:28`) argues for app-layer, but regulated deployers need a harness-provided opt-in transformer (`langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/stream/transformers.py:272` enrichment point).
- What is the retention contract for the forensic ledger? `langgraph` `THREAT_MODEL.md:DC3` notes unbounded retention with no default TTL; `crewai` `human_feedback_history` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/flow/runtime/__init__.py:604`) and `letta` `MemoryRecord.source` (`lib/crewai/src/crewai/memory/types.py:60`) have no purge policy. A governed harness needs `BaseCheckpointSaver` bounded wrapper + `export_audit_log` rather than raw `get_state_history`.
- How should tool-author attribution be surfaced at runtime? `letta` `letta/schemas/tool.py:67` `created_by_id` and `pydantic-ai` `pydantic_ai_slim/pydantic_ai/tools.py:605` `metadata`/`capability_id` exist but are not joined to `ToolCallMessage`/`ToolCallItem`/`ActionEvent` in the public API — what is the minimal non-leaking field set?
- Can the experimental `langgraph` `THREAT_MODEL.md:405` responsibility matrix be promoted to a versioned `ACCOUNTABILITY.md` with enforcement-point `file:line` links, and should other harnesses adopt the same artifact rather than scattering guidance across `README.md:207`, `TRANSPARENCY_FAQ.md:49`, `TERMS.md:14`, and `SECURITY.md`?
- How to make `Command` (`langgraph` `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:759`) distinguish `human approval` from `programmatic goto` in the audit log without parsing `Command` fields — is a `command_kind` tag on `CheckpointMetadata.source` sufficient?
- What is the intended authorization story for client-supplied history? `pydantic-ai` `docs/deferred-tools.md:101` and `agent-framework` `docs/decisions/0006-userapproval.md:92` acknowledge forgeability; is the intended fix an HMAC issuance ledger, re-authorization inside the tool body, or both?
