## Executive Summary

No source achieves closed-loop artifact provenance where every deliverable can be traced to its full input set and reproduced bit-for-bit from recorded inputs. All four score in the fragile middle (4-5/10) but for systematically different reasons that reflect product shape, not just maturity.

The common substrate that **does** work is durable execution lineage: workflow checkpoints, history events, and file-per-event logs give strong, replayable control-plane provenance. `agent-framework` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:31-88`), `langgraph` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:38-87`), `openhands` (`openhands/app_server/event/event_service_base.py:85-97`), and `temporal` (`common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:776-922`) each persist a parent-linked chain sufficient to resume execution. Build reproducibility is also universally pursued via committed lockfiles (`python/uv.lock:1-95`, `libs/langgraph/uv.lock:1`, `go.sum:1-10`, `pyproject.toml:23-109`).

What fails everywhere is the **generative data-plane**: LLM prompts, resolved inference options (`seed`, `temperature`, `tools`), tool payloads/results, context injections, approvals, and environment are either emitted only to ephemeral observability (OTel/LangSmith/LiteLLM), stored in filtered metadata, or not recorded at all. Deterministic replay is therefore limited to "resume from checkpoint and re-execute nodes" — not "reproduce identical text/file artifact from same logical inputs." No source runs a CI gate that asserts artifact hash stability across two fresh runs.

## Core Thesis

This dimension bifurcates into two problems with opposite maturity: **execution provenance** (can I resume the run?) is solved by event-sourced checkpoints; **artifact provenance** (can I audit and reproduce the deliverable?) remains ad-hoc because artifacts are treated as side-effects of execution rather than first-class provenance envelopes. The resulting gap — OTel spans not joined to artifact IDs, checkpoint metadata filtered to primitives, LLM options left as optional `seed=None`, and approval chains kept in session history rather than stamped on outputs — is consistent across all four architectures despite different encodings (pickle+base64, JSONL events, delta-channel writes, proto history). Closing it requires a unified, content-hashed artifact envelope, deterministic build attestation, and a CI replay harness; none of the sources have built this loop.

## Approach Models

Three approach clusters emerge:

**Cluster A — Workflow Checkpoint Lineage (agent-framework, langgraph).** Artifact equals persisted execution state. Provenance is the chain of checkpoints / `CheckpointTuple` parent pointers. `agent-framework` adds a versioned envelope (`WorkflowCheckpoint` with `version="1.0"`, `graph_signature_hash`, `previous_checkpoint_id` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:70-88`) and lossless pickle+base64 encoding with allowlisted `RestrictedUnpickler` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint_encoding.py:115-145`). `langgraph` stores `CheckpointMetadata {source, step, parents, run_id}` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:38-87`) and replays via `channels_from_checkpoint` / `achannels_from_checkpoint` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_checkpoint.py:136-226`). Both support `get_state`/`get_state_history` traversal and `update_state` forks. Both deliberately minimize metadata (primitive-only filtering in `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:757-786` vs free-form `metadata dict` in `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:88`).

**Cluster B — Conversation Event Log (openhands).** Artifact equals the conversation. Provenance is the ordered append-only filesystem log: one JSON file per `Event` under `{user_id}/v1_conversations/{conv_hex}/{event.hex}.json` (`openhands/app_server/event/event_service_base.py:85-97`, `openhands/app_server/conversation_paths.py:11-73`) plus `AppConversationInfo`/`StoredConversationMetadata` columns (`openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:110-139`) and tag-riding provenance (`agentprofileid`, `agentprofilerevision`, `acpserver` at `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:42-48`). Tool artifacts ride inside events (browser `recording-{timestamp}.json` at `tools/browser_use/event_storage.py:41-54`). Export is a zip of the log, and `CriticResult.metadata` preserves `event_ids` as reproducibility anchors (`_sdk_inspect/sdk/critic/impl/api/critic.py:120-132`). Observability moves to `litellm_extra_body.metadata` (`openhands/app_server/utils/llm_metadata.py:10-91`) but only for `openhands/` models.

**Cluster C — Durable History Tree (temporal).** Artifact equals the event-sourced workflow history. Provenance is `AppendHistoryNodes` with `BranchToken` + monotonic `PrevTransactionID`/`TransactionID` and `VersionHistoryItem{EventId, Version}` (`common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:776-922`, `common/persistence/versionhistory/version_history_item.go:12`), enabling branching via `ForkHistoryBranchRequest` and full-branch `ReadHistoryBranch`. Determinism is a documented contract ("Workflow code must be deterministic" at `docs/architecture/README.md:35`) with an opt-in `WithDeterministicProto3` codec (`common/persistence/serialization/codec.go:43-91`). Build provenance is runtime-introspected via `debug.ReadBuildInfo` into `InfoData{GitRevision, GitTime, GoArch}` (`common/build/build.go:29-56`) and OCI labels (`docker/docker-bake.hcl:57-67`).

All three share the same blind spot: **LLM-specific contributing factors are out-of-band**. `agent-framework` emits `SEED`, `REQUEST_MODEL`, `SYSTEM_INSTRUCTIONS` as OTel span attributes (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/observability.py:191-244`) without linking back to `response_id`. `langgraph` delegates to LangSmith callbacks (`libs/langgraph/tests/test_tracing_interops.py:11-117`). `openhands` injects `litellm_extra_body.metadata` only when `openhands/` model prefix matches (`openhands/app_server/utils/llm_metadata.py:10-91`). `temporal` is not an LLM harness and stores `StartWorkflow` payloads as opaque `DataBlob` — prompt provenance is the client's responsibility.

## Pattern Catalog

### Pattern 1 — Parent-Linked Checkpoint Chain with Content Hash

*Problem:* Detect topology drift and enable linear audit without scanning full history.
*Sources:* `agent-framework` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_workflow.py:1015-1082` canonical JSON + SHA256 `graph_signature_hash`; `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner_context.py:370-395` chaining `previous_checkpoint_id`), `langgraph` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:139-146` `parent_config` + `InMemorySaver.put` `parent_checkpoint_id` at `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/memory/__init__.py:427-471`), `temporal` (`common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:776-922` `BranchToken` + `TransactionID` chain).
*Why it works:* Constant-time drift check on restore; cheap traversal for `get_state_history`.
*When to copy:* Any durable workflow or agent harness where resume/fork matters.
*When risky/overkill:* Small single-turn agents without resume; hash computation adds build-time cost if graph is trivial.
*Evidence:* `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:31-88`, `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:38-87`, `common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:776-922`.

### Pattern 2 — Atomic Persistence with Traversal Guard

*Problem:* Prevent torn writes and directory escape in artifact stores.
*Sources:* `agent-framework` `FileCheckpointStorage.save` writes `*.tmp` then `os.replace` with `is_relative_to` guard (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:302-326`, `282-300`); `temporal` `WorkflowMutation{Condition/DBRecordVersion}` optimistic guard (`common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:375-378`) and `.bin` + `.stamp` sentinel installs (`Makefile:179-286`).
*Why it works:* Crash-durability and security without distributed transactions.
*When to copy:* Always for file-based checkpoint/event stores.
*Overkill:* Pure in-memory test stores where durability is not required (hence `InMemoryCheckpointStorage` uses `copy.deepcopy` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:192-211`).
*Evidence:* `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:302-326`, `common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:375-378`.

### Pattern 3 — Allowlist-Gated Deserialization

*Problem:* Preserve fidelity for arbitrary Python/Go types without opening code-execution surface.
*Sources:* `agent-framework` `RestrictedUnpickler.find_class` allowlisting `agent_framework.*`, `openai.types.*`, `module:qualname` keys (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint_encoding.py:115-145`, `309-311`); `langgraph` `JsonPlusSerializer._create_msgpack_ext_hook` with `LANGGRAPH_STRICT_MSGPACK` and `allowed_msgpack_modules` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/serde/jsonplus.py:546-744`).
*Why it works:* Balances human-readable JSON with Python object fidelity; fails closed on unknown types.
*When risky:* Trusted-storage assumption is not a boundary (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint_encoding.py:17-44`); post-deserialization `getattr` paths remain abuse-prone. Require explicit `allowed_checkpoint_types` per custom state type or entire checkpoint becomes unloadable (`python/packages/core/tests/workflow/test_checkpoint.py:1051-1074`).
*Evidence:* `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint_encoding.py:115-145`, `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/serde/jsonplus.py:546-744`.

### Pattern 4 — Determinism Hardening via Sorting and Stable IDs

*Problem:* Map iteration, task ordering, and message IDs introduce nondeterminism across replays.
*Sources:* `langgraph` `prepare_next_tasks` sorts nodes (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:253,481`) and `ensure_message_ids` sanitizes `id=None` BaseMessages before delta writes (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:452-458`); `agent-framework` `State` superstep buffer commits only at boundary (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_state.py:6-127`) and `WorkflowMessage` propagates `trace_contexts: list[dict[str,str]]` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner_context.py:36-62`); `temporal` sorts updates before emission (`service/worker/batcher/activities.go:68`) and documents "Sort by request type for deterministic output."
*Why it works:* Eliminates most scheduler-level nondeterminism.
*When insufficient:* Wall-clock `ts`, UUIDs, and `random.getrandbits`/`random.random()` still break byte stability (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/id.py:79-108`, `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/memory/__init__.py:619-628`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_retry.py:672,828`, `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_agents.py:755-778` `seed` defaults to `None`).
*Evidence:* `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:253`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:452-458`, `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_state.py:6-127`.

### Pattern 5 — Committed Lockfiles with Constraint Overrides

*Problem:* Reproduce builds from same inputs across platforms.
*Sources:* `agent-framework` `python/uv.lock` v1+hashes + `pyproject.toml:55-68` `constraint-dependencies`/`override-dependencies` + `supported-markers` darwin/linux/win32; `langgraph` monorepo `pyproject.toml`/`uv.lock` per lib with `hatchling`; `openhands` `pyproject.toml:23-109` exact pins (`orjson==3.11.8`, `litellm==1.84.1`) + `poetry.lock` + `uv.lock` + `Dockerfile:24,78-86` `POETRY_VERSION=2.3.4`/`pip==26.0.1`; `temporal` `go.mod:3` `go 1.26.4` + `go.sum` `h1:` hashes + `Makefile:179-276` pinned `GOLANGCI_LINT v2.13.0`/ `BUF v1.6.0` via `.stamp` sentinels.
*Why it works:* Single diff to review; per-platform marker resolution.
*When risky:* Single shared `uv.lock` couples providers (blast radius); floating Alpine/CUDA bases without digest still drift (`docker/docker-bake.hcl:40-42` tag-only).
*Evidence:* `python/uv.lock:1-95`, `python/pyproject.toml:55-68`, `pyproject.toml:23-109`, `containers/app/Dockerfile:24,78-86`, `go.mod:3`, `go.sum:1-10`, `Makefile:179-286`.

### Pattern 6 — Tag-Riding Provenance for Zero-Migration Evolution

*Problem:* Add provenance fields without DB migrations.
*Sources:* `openhands` `AppConversationInfo.tags: dict[str,str]` with `agentprofileid`/`agentprofilerevision`/`acpserver`/`archiveworkspacepath` and `@computed_field` projections (`openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:42-48,140-178`); `langgraph` `patch_checkpoint_map`/`merge_configs` threading `checkpoint_map`/`parents` through `RunnableConfig` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/_internal/_config.py:63-80,147-191`).
*Why it works:* Fast iteration.
*When risky:* Schema is implicit; malformed `tags['agentprofileid']` silently maps to `return None` (`openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:176-178`); `get_checkpoint_metadata` silently drops non-primitives and `__`-prefixed keys (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:797-807`).
*Evidence:* `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:42-48,140-178`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/_internal/_config.py:63-80`.

### Pattern 7 — Externalized Observability as Provisional Provenance

*Problem:* Capture model/tool/approval signals without inflating persisted artifact.
*Sources:* `agent-framework` OTel `OtelAttr` `SEED`/`REQUEST_MODEL`/`TEMPERATURE`/`SYSTEM_INSTRUCTIONS` gated by `enableSensitiveData` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/observability.py:191-244,682-812`); `langgraph` LangSmith `LangChainTracer`/`FakeTracer` (`libs/langgraph/tests/test_tracing_interops.py:11-117`); `openhands` `litellm_extra_body.metadata` trace tags (`openhands/app_server/utils/llm_metadata.py:10-91`); `temporal` `client-name`/`client-version` header propagation (`common/headers/version_checker.go:24-60`).
*Why it works:* Cheap, low-bloat, leverages existing tracing infra.
*When risky:* Offline audits require joining traces to artifacts out-of-band; `openhands/` prefix gate drops third-party model provenance; spans not linked to `response_id`/`checkpoint_id` by default; OTel without exporter loses provenance entirely.
*Evidence:* `python/packages/core/agent_framework/observability.py:191-244`, `libs/langgraph/tests/test_tracing_interops.py:11-117`, `openhands/app_server/utils/llm_metadata.py:10-91`.

### Pattern 8 — Delta/Exit-Mode Storage Optimization

*Problem:* Reduce write amplification for fine-grained channel writes.
*Sources:* `langgraph` delta channels scattered across `checkpoint_writes` and reconstructed via `get_delta_channel_history` ancestor walk (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_checkpoint.py:136-184`, `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:582-649`, `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/memory/__init__.py:142-229`) with `Durability = "sync"|"async"|"exit"` (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:87-93`); `temporal` `HistoryStatistics.SizeDiff` and `DeleteHistoryBranch`/`TrimHistoryBranch` lifecycle (`common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:771-774,947-968`).
*Why it works:* Efficient for high-frequency loops.
*When risky:* Pruning an intermediate `checkpoint_writes` or sole `_DeltaSnapshot` silently returns empty channels (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:331-415` advisory warnings, not enforced); `Durability="exit"` can lose sub-frequency inputs on crash (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:697-703,1006-1013`); temporal archival erases provenance.
*Evidence:* `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_checkpoint.py:136-184`, `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:331-415`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:697-703`.

## Key Differences

**What converges:** All four converge on durable lineage + pinned builds as the provenance backbone, and all four fail the same four checks from the dimension: no unified artifact envelope, no deterministic artifact replay, incomplete contributing-factor capture, and no CI reproducibility gate. Every per-source answers section records `No` or `Partially` for "Can every artifact be traced to its inputs?" and "Is reproducibility tested in CI?" The pattern is not language-specific (Python `uv.lock` and Go `go.sum` behave identically here).

**Why they diverge — product shape, not quality:**

- *Artifact definition drives storage.* `agent-framework` and `langgraph` are agent harnesses where the artifact is the workflow state; they invest in checkpoint codecs and lineage. `openhands` is a coding agent where the artifact is the trajectory + derived files; it invests in `Event` filesystem logs and trajectory export. `temporal` is a durable execution server where the artifact is the history tree; it invests in `BranchToken` branching. Each correctly optimizes for its own artifact, but none generalize to file/patch artifacts produced by LLM tool calls.

- *Hosted vs. embedded observability.* `agent-framework` and `temporal` emit build/execution metadata from the runtime itself (`debug.ReadBuildInfo` at `common/build/build.go:29-56` vs. OTel spans at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/observability.py:191-244`). `langgraph` and `openhands` depend on external SaaS tracing (LangSmith, Laminar via LiteLLM) that must be separately joined — a valid choice for library-vs-hosted constraints but one that makes offline provenance incomplete by default.

- *Security vs. fidelity tradeoff is handled differently.* `agent-framework` accepts pickle fidelity and mitigates with `RestrictedUnpickler` and trusted-storage caveat (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint_encoding.py:17-44`). `langgraph` opts for `JsonPlusSerializer`/`ormsgpack` with strict `allowlist` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/serde/jsonplus.py:258-290`). `temporal` avoids the problem by using deterministic proto marshaling (`common/persistence/serialization/codec.go:43-91`) — a Go-idiomatic choice that is not available to Python's dynamic types.

- *Build distribution assumptions.* All pin deps, but image provenance diverges: `temporal` and `openhands` bake OCI labels (`docker/docker-bake.hcl:57-67`, `containers/app/Dockerfile:38,46`) while `agent-framework`/`langgraph` rely on `uv.lock` alone without image SBOM/attestation. Only enterprise `openhands` (`enterprise-server`) enables Docker provenance+SBOM (`.github/workflows/ghcr-build.yml:41-43`); OSS builds across all sources omit SLSA attestations entirely. Temporal explicitly rejects Alpine digest pinning for multi-arch correctness (`docker/docker-bake.hcl:39`).

## Decision Guide

**If you are building a workflow/step harness (agent-framework/langgraph-shaped):**
- Start with Cluster A: versioned checkpoint envelope with `previous_checkpoint_id` chain, `graph_signature_hash`, and atomic `tmp`→`replace`. Copy `State` superstep semantics (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_state.py:6-127`) to avoid mid-superstep pollution.
- Add the artifact envelope on day one: `inputs_fingerprint` (hash of messages + merged `ChatOptions` + tool registry versions + `graph_signature_hash`) stamped on `AgentResponse`/`WorkflowRunResult` output events. Retrofitting this after tracing-only provenance (as all four learned) is expensive.

**If you are building a coding/conversation agent (openhands-shaped):**
- Start with Cluster B: file-per-event log (`openhands/app_server/event/event_service_base.py:85-97`) plus export zip. Keep `AppConversationInfo` explicit for queryability and tags only for low-cardinality provenance; do not overload `tags` for approval or per-turn LLM params.
- Pin `ref` → SHA at `_build_start_conversation_request_for_user` time (`openhands/app_server/app_conversation/live_status_app_conversation_service.py:1162-1250`) and record base image digest alongside `AgentProfile` revision. This closes the "skill version can drift" gap noted at `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:71-81`.

**If you are building a durable execution platform (temporal-shaped):**
- Start with Cluster C: `BranchToken` + `TransactionID` chaining plus deterministic codec opt-in (`common/persistence/serialization/codec.go:43-91`). Document determinism as a contract (`docs/architecture/README.md:35`) and enforce via `workflowRebuilder`.
- Promote `timestamp()` labels to `vcs.time` (`common/build/build.go:46-53`) and add `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH` + `-trimpath` for reproducible images; do not rely on `debug.ReadBuildInfo` alone (it is unavailable on `go run` at `common/build/build.go:30-32`).

**Choosing between minimal vs. rich checkpoint metadata:**
- Minimal (LangGraph-style `EXCLUDED_METADATA_KEYS` at `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:797-807`) when checkpoint write throughput dominates cost and you have external tracing to absorb richness.
- Rich (agent-framework `metadata dict` or OpenHands `StoredConversationMetadata` columns at `openhands/app_server/app_conversation/sql_app_conversation_info_service.py:132-190`) when offline audit without trace joins is required.
- Decision rule: if your artifacts leave the tracing VPC (user-downloaded zips, hosted files, published packages), embed; if they never leave, externalize.

**Choosing between time-based and content-based artifact IDs:**
- Time-based (`uuid6` at `libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/id.py:79-108`) when concurrent writer ordering and DB locality dominate.
- Content-based (hash of `channel_values`/`Event` payload + `seed`) when reproducibility asserts and `diff`-stable artifacts matter. Enable a deterministic mode for CI (seed `random`, disable jitter at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_retry.py:672`, replace `ts` with `vcs.time`) without changing production defaults.

## Practical Tips

**Patterns to copy now:**
1. **Atomic file writes** (`*.tmp` → `os.replace` at `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:317-321`) and **optimistic `DBRecordVersion` guards** (`common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:375-378`) — cheap, no downside.
2. **Graph/canonical hash on every checkpoint** (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_workflow.py:1015-1082`) — catches drift before replay.
3. **Committed lockfiles with constraint overrides** (`python/pyproject.toml:55-68`, `go.sum:1-10`) plus **pinned tool installs via `.stamp` sentinels** (`Makefile:179-286`) — makes build repro a one-command check.
4. **`ensure-no-changes` git-porcelain gate** (`Makefile:806-810`) for generated code — partial input→output consistency at almost zero cost.
5. **Deterministic task/message ordering** (`libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_algo.py:253`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_loop.py:452-458`) even when full byte determinism is out of reach.

**Patterns to delay until needed:**
- Delta-channel scatter (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/memory/__init__.py:142-229`) and `Durability="exit"` — only when checkpoint write volume justifies complexity; otherwise superstep atomic writes are sufficient.
- Full SBOM/SLSA attestations per build — prioritize `checksums.txt` (` .goreleaser.yml:88-90`) and OCI labels first; add `syft`/`cosign` once OSS builds need supply-chain verification.

**Decision rules:**
- If `seed` is not systematically fixed, do not promise artifact determinism; gate deterministic demos on `ChatOptions(seed=0)` + deterministic mock tools and assert `hash(outputs)` stable in a dedicated CI job (proposed in `agent-framework` future but missing everywhere).
- If `approval` gates exist, persist `approver` + `timestamp` + `rule_id` on the output envelope (all four report approval as missing beyond session/history: `python/packages/core/agent_framework/_harness/_tool_approval.py:120-140`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/types.py:811-934`, `schemas/durable-agent-entity-state.json:1`, `common/headers/headers.go:32-42`).
- If you use `pyproject.toml` `supports` markers (`python/uv.lock:4-23`) or Alpine tags (`docker/docker-bake.hcl:40-42`), record the resolved marker/tag + digest alongside the artifact, not just the constraint — otherwise cross-platform attribution is lost.

**Caution signs the design is becoming brittle:**
- `list_checkpoints` warnings swallowed while `load` raises (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:372-388`) — listing appears healthier than store.
- `get_delta_channel_history` needing ancestor walk with no `seed` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:582-649`) — indicates fragility after prune.
- `tags` free-form dict silently dropping malformed values (`openhands/app_server/app_conversation/app_conversation_models.py:176-178`) or `get_checkpoint_metadata` dropping non-primitives (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:757-786`) — signals missing schema enforcement.
- `build.InfoData.Available=false` on `go run`/`go test` (`common/build/build.go:30-32`) — any check reading `GitRevision` as `""` without error is a silent provenance hole.
- Large blast-radius `uv.lock` churn on Dependabot updates (no per-package bisection) — indicates a single-lock hazard noted in `agent-framework` tradeoffs.

## Anti-Patterns / Caution Signs

1. **Trace-only provenance.** Storing prompts/model/tool calls only in OTel/LangSmith/LiteLLM without a durable artifact-bound copy. Symptom: `raw_representation` on `AgentResponse` not stamped with span `trace_id`; re-exporting the checkpoint/event log yields no LLM provenance (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_types.py:393-415`, `libs/langgraph/tests/test_tracing_interops.py:11-117`, `openhands/app_server/utils/llm_metadata.py:10-91`).

2. **Filtered metadata as provenance.** Relying on `get_checkpoint_metadata` primitive-only allowlist (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:769-774`) or empty-by-default `WorkflowCheckpoint.metadata` (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_runner_context.py:370-395`) to answer "what inputs produced this artifact?" — guaranteed incompleteness.

3. **Non-deterministic artifact bytes treated as reproducible.** Mixing `uuid6`/wall-clock `ts` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:92-124`, `libs/langgraph/langgraph/pregel/_checkpoint.py:80`) and `random.random()` version suffixes (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/memory/__init__.py:619-628`) with claims of replay determinism — replay correctness ≠ byte reproducibility.

4. **Silent corruption recovery.** `FileCheckpointStorage.list_checkpoints` logging `WARNING` and continuing (`python/packages/core/agent_framework/_workflows/_checkpoint.py:372-388`), `BaseCheckpointSaver.prune` advisory warnings (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:374-415`), and `build.InfoData.Available=false` without failure — all mask data loss.

5. **Build provenance that is wall-clock sensitive.** `docker/docker-bake.hcl:64,91` `created=timestamp()` plus `ALPINE_TAG` without digest (`docker/docker-bake.hcl:40-42`) ensures two rebuilds on same commit diverge; `ServerVersion = "1.32.0"` (`common/headers/version_checker.go:24-60`) further collapses per-commit granularity to tag.

6. **Enterprise-only SBOM/provenance** (`.github/workflows/_build-image.yml:26-32`, `.github/workflows/ghcr-build.yml:42-43`). OSS consumers cannot verify builder identity or dependency closure — anti-pattern for supply-chain reproducibility.

7. **History pruning without delta awareness.** Deleting intermediate `checkpoint_writes` or trimming `BranchToken` history (`common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:947-968`) when DeltaChannel or `get_delta_channel_history` semantics require the ancestor seed — silent empty-channel reconstruction.

## Open Questions

1. Do maintainers intend to support offline verification of a hosted file/data-plane artifact back to its prompt/model/approval without querying traces? Current code treats hosting stores as trusted application storage rather than audited provenance ledgers (agent-framework `FileAccessProvider`, openhands `recording-{timestamp}.json`).

2. Is SLSA level 1-2 attestation intentionally deferred, or just backlog? No `.slsa` config, `slsa-framework/slsa-github-generator`, or `actions/attest-build-provenance` found in any `.github/`; no ADL/ADR explains omission (raised in temporal gaps, equally applicable to others).

3. Should environment factors (`LANGGRAPH_DEFAULT_RECURSION_LIMIT`/`DELTA_MAX_SUPERSTEPS_SINCE_SNAPSHOT` at `libs/langgraph/langgraph/_internal/_config.py:32-34`, `TEMPORAL_TEST_DATA_ENCODING` at `Makefile:91`, dynamic config `development-*.yaml`) be part of per-artifact provenance? They affect generated artifacts but are ephemeral.

4. Who owns the provenance envelope for LLM calls when the harness is a library? `langgraph` and `agent-framework` delegate to `RunnableConfig.callbacks`/`OTel` (`libs/langgraph/tests/test_tracing_interops.py:68-117`, `python/packages/core/agent_framework/observability.py:176-282`); `temporal` not at all. Is the expectation that the *client* records `{prompt_hash, model_id, tool_call_ids}` and passes it in `StartWorkflow`/`Invoke` payload, or that the harness should persist it atomically with the checkpoint as proposed (`CheckpointMetadata.provenance: {input_hash, prompt_hash, model_id}`)?

5. How long must `BranchToken`/`checkpoint_writes` history be retained for old runs to remain traceable? `temporal` `DeleteHistoryBranch`/`TrimHistoryBranch` (`common/persistence/data_interfaces.go:947-968`) and `langgraph` `BaseCheckpointSaver.prune`/`delete_for_runs` (`libs/checkpoint/langgraph/checkpoint/base/__init__.py:331-415`) trade storage cost for provenance durability without documented retention at artifact level.

6. For durable/hosted variants (`packages/durabletask`, `schemas/durable-agent-entity-state.json`), is the orchestration's own checkpoint store the system of record, or should the in-proc `WorkflowCheckpoint` lineage be surfaced symmetrically (agent-framework future consideration)?
